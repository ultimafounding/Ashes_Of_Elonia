## As Played

### 1. The Salt-Lantern
- The party noticed **the Breakers** in the corner. A successful **Investigation** check established them as a threat, not just dockhands.
- The table got **very drunk**.
- A **natural 20 on the Persuasion pitch** to Helena meant she bought **everything the party wanted**: **6 ration packs, 2 healing potions, and a lantern**. She also paid the **advance** (15 gp each unless the table negotiated up to 20 — confirm).
- The next morning **James and Kit were badly hung over and late**. Helena was visibly annoyed — but she honoured the deal and paid for the gear.
- **James** wandered off to look at "shinies" for a long time.
- **Theo** failed to convince Helena to buy the party beer.

### 2. The city
- The party listened to the **street prophet**. They got bored when he stopped ranting at the street and started rambling **at Dan about "the aura coming off him."**
- They headed for the Warrens. **Theo** was distracted by a pub and got drunk again — mechanically **inebriated / disadvantage** for the delve (DM ruling).

### 3. Warehouse Nine
- They looked around the warehouse and found the **sliced rope** on a decent **Investigation** check. **Confirmed: deliberately cut — and not by the Breakers.** A **third party**, acting in a hurry, severed the line to **hide evidence of tampering**. This is a cover-up, not a Cartel betrayal. *(DM note: Tamsin was stranded by someone outside the Breakers. The cut should read as rushed — not a clean single stroke. The open question is **what was tampered with**: the dust room, the hidden chests, the records, or the brass door.)*
- They went down with **Arlen**, who managed to **really piss the players off** almost immediately.
- **Helena stayed up top**, nursing a hangover and annoyed at having had to buy everything. The players know the **salvage vs. antiquities** rules.

### 4. Cellar rats
- **Dan's Perception** spotted the rats before they could ambush the party. No disadvantage except **Theo/Bog** (drunk).
- The rats were annihilated — except **one**, which got through and dropped **James/Zook to 2 HP**.
- **The fight was loud.** It carried (see clocks).
- The party jumped down through the hatch. **They did not close it behind them.**
- After the fight, **Dan healed Zook with *Cure Wounds*** (one 1st-level slot spent). No potion was used.

### 5. The descent (improvised)
- The drop opened onto a **small ledge overlooking a scree pit**. Nobody realised it was scree until they were on it; climbing down called for a **Dex save**.
- Everyone balanced on the ledge. On the rope, **Kit failed the save and fell** — **Theo/Bog made a Strength check and caught him** on the way down.
- *(This replaces the prep's three-pitch Catalogue Descent. It is now table canon.)*

### 6. The archive room (still 1.1)
- The party explored a **library-like room** and were surprised there was **no murderous cult imagery** waiting for them.
- **Statues** caught them off guard. **James** was fascinated by the workmanship.
- **Kit** looked at the books and found **grain shipments and municipal codes** — not scripture. **Arlen was beside himself; his worldview took a visible hit.**
- **Dan** tried to pick up a book; it **crumbled**.
- This room is the table's expanded version of **1.1's civic archive imagery** — Clue A landed hard, earlier and more personally than the prep expected.

### 7. The centipede ambush
- **Arlen pushed forward** despite being, in the table's words, "incredibly pathetic," and was ambushed by **two centipedes**.
- The party **nearly left him to die** — they hate him — but **Dan jumped between Arlen and a centipede**, and the others joined in.
- **No centipede hit a PC, and the fight was over quickly.** No clock tick for this one. Arlen survived.

### 8. The door to the dust room
- At the very end, the door opened onto the **dust room** — the Hall of Records (1.2).
- **The session ended there.** 1.2 is still unexplored; the ghoul, the skeletons, the Breaker camp, the dust furrow, and the ledger leaf are all still ahead.

## Divergences From Prep / Canon Impacts

1. **Helena bought the gear on a nat 20.** Two healing potions retail at 100 gp — a huge outlay for a level-1 charter. This makes her more invested (or more humiliated) than the prep assumed. Use it.
2. **The prophet read Dan's aura.** He stopped preaching at the street and spoke directly to Dan. Do not explain it. This is now a live Dan/Selyra thread.
3. **The rope was deliberately sliced — by a third party.** Confirmed: someone outside the Breakers cut it in a hurry to cover evidence of tampering. This introduces an unknown actor, turns the cut into a cover-up rather than a Cartel betrayal, and means Tamsin was stranded by someone else. What they tampered with is still the live question.
4. **1.2 is untouched.** The ghoul, skeletons, dying Breaker, dust furrow, survey chit, and ledger leaf are all still ahead. Session 2 can run as written, with the cut rope added as fresh evidence.
5. **Arlen is hated, not just disliked.** The prep wanted the party to like him "a little less"; the table went to open contempt. Dan's intervention is the one warm beat. Decide whether to lean into the comedy, give Arlen a redemption beat, or both.
6. **The party expanded 1.1.** They explored an improvised archive room, learned the civic-not-cult truth early, and had a strong Clue A moment. Session 2 should not re-teach that reveal; let the ledger leaf deepen it instead.
7. **Hidden chests in the dust room.** The party knows they exist but has not touched them. They are now a Session 2 objective; decide what is inside before play. **Likely link:** the chests are the obvious thing the third party tampered with before cutting the rope — confirm before play.
8. **No physical loot yet.** The party is carrying notes and rubbings, not salvage. Helena's 10% cut has nothing to take a percentage of.
