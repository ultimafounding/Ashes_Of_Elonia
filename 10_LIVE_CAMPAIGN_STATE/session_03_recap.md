# Session 3 — The Oath in the Dust (Actual Play)

> *"We keep account, we mend bone, we measure star — that no prayer need be spent for bread."*

**Prep:** [[session_03_the_oath]] • **Next:** Chapter 2
**Ended:** in a Port Meridian pub — everyone except Arlen; Tamsin is with the party

> **Status:** running log. Entries marked **[confirm]** need a ruling before they become canon.

---

## As Played

### 1. The brass door
- They listened at the door: **the hiss**, and **pry marks** around the seal.
- Inside, they found the **other half of the snapped lockpick** in the seal.
- **Bog ripped it out.** The seal failed. Dry air flooded the Scriptorium, and **all loose paper not stored in the bookshelves was destroyed**.
- The Oath itself is cut into stone, so it survived. The loose documents did not.

### 2. The Scriptorium
- **Arlen read the arch** — the Oath inscription. It landed.
- **Zook fawned over the copper**, grabbed it, and **hoarded it**. He is now carrying the party's heaviest loot.
- The room's remaining paper was ruined by the seal failure; the shelves and the stone survived.

### 3. The dead Breaker
- They found **Wren**, dead, **his soul ripped out**. The party was genuinely worried.
- **Tamsin recognised him** — his survey partner, the man he thought was lost.
- **Dan stole Wren's coat** — a "sick ass coat" that gives **+1 Charisma** because it is just that cool.

### 4. The cellar
- They went into the **cellar** and found **another Breaker** in there — not Wren.
- They fought him, **nearly killed him, then tied him up**.
- They **stole the Catalogue Index Tablet and a leaf from him** — almost certainly the missing **ledger leaf**.
- **Zook also took a Bag of Holding off him.** That is how the copper is travelling.
- The party did **not** give Tamsin up to the authorities.
- They brought the captured Breaker out and **handed him to the magistrates**. He is alive and in custody. His name is still unknown.

### 5. The collapse
- As they went up, the **ceiling collapsed**. **Three of them fell**, but **everyone got out safely**, including the captured Breaker.
- The Scriptorium is gone or sealed behind them.

### 6. The surface
- On the surface, they spotted **Arlen carrying "the precious stone"** — not the Tablet. The party kept the Tablet.
- **Helena questioned him**, and the party **forced him to throw the precious stone back**.
- **Arlen confessed (Clue C).** Then he **left after threatening the players** — but he is not a villain here, just being a dick.
- **Helena caught a glimpse of the party's loot but let it go.** They are okay; no consequence.
- The session ended with **everyone except Arlen going to the pub**. **Tamsin is with the party.**

1. **Wren was the dead Breaker.** His soul was ripped out, Tamsin recognised him, and Dan took his coat. This is a direct, visceral Soul Crisis manifestation — do not explain the mechanism yet.
2. **The captured Breaker is someone else.** Unnamed, alive, handed to the magistrates. He was the one holding the Tablet, leaf, and Bag of Holding; he had already looted the Scriptorium.
3. **The Tablet and leaf came from the captured Breaker**, not the desk.
4. **Bog destroyed the loose paper** by ripping the pick out of the seal. The Oath survived on stone; the documents did not.
5. **Arlen confessed (Clue C), then left after a petty threat.** He was carrying **"the precious stone"** — not the Tablet — and was forced to throw it back. He is being a dick, not becoming a villain.
6. **Tamsin is with the party at the pub.** He recognised Wren's body and was **not** handed over to the magistrates. He is a companion with a target on his back.
