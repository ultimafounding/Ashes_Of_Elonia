# Session 2 — What the Dust Remembers (Actual Play)

> *"Dust lies like snow. Something has walked here."*

**Prep:** [[session_02_hall_of_records]] • **Next:** [[session_03_the_oath]] — the brass door
**Ended:** at the brass door, with Tamsin Roke in tow

---

## As Played

### 1. Into the dust
- The party entered the Hall of Records **carefully** — dust discipline held.
- **Bog threw the gnome (Zook) at the grate.** It shifted some dust, but not enough to destroy the room's evidence.
- The grate **could not be opened** — a **critical failure**. Whatever was under it stayed under it.

### 2. The Breaker camp
- They crossed the Hall and reached the **Breaker camp**.
- **Dan cast *Spare the Dying*** on the dying Breaker. **Tamsin Roke survived.**
- Tamsin gave them **information about Rorik** and the **glass-faced ghosts**. That is the Soul Crisis rumour in play; it still has no mechanics and must not be explained.
- They found the **snapped steel lockpick half** — modern steel, proof someone contemporary worked the brass door. The party understands the message: **someone modern has been here.**

### 3. The ghoul
- They fought and killed the **ghoul**.
- **Bog was downed** in the fight, made his **death saving throw**, and survived. He did **not** die.
- **Tamsin lost a leg** to the ghoul.

### 4. Aftermath and long rest
- **Zook built Tamsin a peg leg.** The artificer solved a problem with tools and time; the Breaker is mobile again.
- The party took a **long rest in the Hall**. HP and spell slots are restored.
- **Tamsin came with them** as a companion.
- They picked up **a couple of beers** from the camp and carried on.

### 5. The chest
- They found a **chest** in the dust. The furrow had avoided it, so they suspected a **mimic** and smashed it.
- The chest itself was **not tampered with**. It was just a chest.
- Inside were an **eyepatch** and a **pink scarf**.
- **John Fantasy put on the scarf** — a **+1 Constitution magic scarf of protection** (homebrew).
- **Bog put on the eyepatch — and the eyepatch was the mimic.** It tried to attach; Bog made his save, **threw it off, and it ran away.** It may turn up again.

### 6. The brass door
- They reached the **brass door** with Tamsin's information and a new companion.
- Session ended there. The door itself is Session 3's opening.

## Divergences / Canon Impacts

1. **Tamsin Roke lives and travels with the party.** He is a **new companion** with a peg leg, not just a recurring informant.
2. **Zook built the peg leg.** A strong artificer moment; it makes Tamsin physically dependent on Zook and gives the party a real tie to him.
3. **Bog survived a downing.** The party has now had two near-deaths at level 1 (Zook in Session 1, Bog here). Telegraph danger harder.
4. **The mimic escaped.** It is at large in the Hall and may return.
5. **The chest was mundane.** It was not the third party's tampering target. The only modern-intrusion trace they found is the **snapped lockpick half**.
6. **Clue B is still loose.** They did **not** find or translate the ledger leaf.
7. **The grate critical failure.** Whatever was under it is still there. It is a live loose end.
8. **The third party's tampering is still undefined.** They cut the rope to hide evidence, but the party has not found what was tampered with. The brass door is the obvious candidate.
