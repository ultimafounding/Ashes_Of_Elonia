Open exactly where you stopped.

> *Two feet of dust. Rows of stone desks. One furrow, straight as a ruled line, running from the doorway to the far dark. And from that dark, the sound of a fingernail drawn across paper.*

Ask the table, in order:
- *"Who steps in first, and how?"*

**Do not** roll initiative. The sound is not yet a monster — it is a question.

## 3. Into the Dust (45 min)

This is the exploration beat. Do not summarize the room — let them search it. The Hall is **90 ft by 40 ft**, with a 20-ft ceiling and a collapsed mezzanine at the far end.

### 3.1 Dust discipline `[DM-ONLY — the room's core rule]`

The dust is **two feet deep** and preserves everything. That cuts both ways.

- Moving at **half speed or less**: no tracks. They can read the floor.
- Moving at **normal speed**: they kick up a knee-high cloud. **DC 10 Survival** to re-read any tracks they cross; on a failure, that section is smeared.
- **Running, fighting, or casting a spell with a verbal component**: the cloud is total in a 10-ft radius until the end of the next round. Any tracks there are **destroyed**.

**Tell the players this up front.** It turns the room into a choice: rush and lose the evidence, or creep and lose time. Helena's clock is the pressure.

### 3.2 The Hall key

| Area | What's there | Checks / payoffs |
|------|--------------|------------------|
| **A. The lectern rows** | Thirty stone desks in dead-straight lines, each holding a fused stack of leaves — petrified parchment, grey as slate. | **DC 12 Investigation:** the leaves are *stacked in order*, not scattered. A filing system. **DC 14 History/Religion:** the catalog marks are numerals, not scripture. |
| **B. The index wall** | A grid of clay niches, each with a labelled tag on a cord. Most tags are blank or missing. | **DC 13 Investigation:** roughly one niche in five is **empty** — and the dust inside those niches is *older* than the room. Something was taken long ago. **Do not explain.** |
| **C. The relief frieze** | Scholars in robes with compasses, plumb-lines, and astrolabes, measuring orbits. No altars, no skulls. | **DC 11 History/Religion:** civic astronomy, not worship. Reinforces Clue A. |
| **D. The collapsed mezzanine** | A spiral stair to a half-floor that fell in. A gap of 15 ft to the surviving ledge. | **DC 13 Athletics/Acrobatics** to climb. From the ledge: the whole room, the far door, and the **ghoul's shape** in the dark before anyone else sees it. Reward the climb with early warning. |
| **E. The dry sump** | A square cistern, bone-dry, crusted with lime. A rusted grate at the bottom. | **DC 12 Perception:** something metal glints under the grate. **DC 14 Athletics** to lift it. It's a bronze **catalog rod** — a 3-ft measuring rod with graduation marks. Salvage (15 gp) and a useful straightedge. |
| **F. The Breaker camp** | A bedroll, a dead lamp, an oilskin, a half-eaten ration, and a **survey chit** pinned under a boot. | This is where the ghoul is feeding. See §3.3. |
| **G. The far door** | Round brass, green with age, half-latched. The source of the hiss. | Session 3. Do not open it tonight. |

**Arlen's running commentary:** he is delighted, then increasingly quiet. Let him identify the catalog numerals, the filing system, the fact that this is an *administrative* room — and let him go silent when the party reaches the Breaker camp.

### 3.3 The Breaker camp — first contact

> *A bedroll. A lamp on its side, dry. An oilskin folded with a surveyor's neatness. And beyond it, in the dust, a shape that is mostly a man, and a second shape that is not.*

**The ghoul is gorging.** It has dragged a Breaker into the lee of a lectern and is eating. It has not noticed the party yet — **passive Perception 10**. If the party stays at the doorway and speaks quietly, they get a full round to plan. If anyone moves at normal speed in the dust, it looks up.

**The dying Breaker.** The man on the floor is not dead. He is unconscious at **0 HP**, stable for now (the ghoul is not finished). If the party stabilizes him — DC 10 Medicine, *Spare the Dying*, a healer's kit, or *Cure Wounds* — he wakes enough to talk. See §4.4.

**The survey chit** (handout, end of file): a cartel split-coin mark, a date three weeks old, and a line of cramped writing: *"W9 — lower gallery — R."* This is the environmental proof of the modern intrusion. **Do not hide it in a check** — let them find it on the body.

**If they search the camp before the fight:** the ghoul attacks. It is not stealthy when surprised.

## 4. The Furrow — Investigation (25 min)

This beat can run **before** the ghoul (if they sneak past) or **after** (if they fight). Either way, it is the session's core revelation.

### 4.1 What the dust holds

| Evidence | Check | What it says |
|----------|-------|--------------|
| **Fresh iron boot-heel marks** | DC 13 Survival or Investigation (automatic if they move at half speed) | A modern boot, nailed heel, size and gait of a grown man. **Over** the ancient dust, not under it. Someone walked here within weeks. |
| **A snapped steel lockpick** | DC 12 Investigation, or automatic when crossing the far third | Modern steel, not bronze. The tip is still in the brass door's mechanism. |
| **A grease-rag** | DC 12 Investigation, or automatic with **Detect Magic**/a good nose | Smells of **New Ilya lamp oil** — violet-white, distinct from Coast whale oil. The same oil the Breakers bought at the Salt-Lantern. |
| **The furrow itself** | No check | Straight as a survey line, from the doorway to the far door. Whoever walked it was *working*, not wandering. |
| **A second set of prints** | DC 15 Investigation (optional) | Smaller, barefoot, and they *stop* at the ghoul's camp. They do not leave. **Say nothing.** It might be a previous victim. It might not. |

### 4.2 Arlen's reaction `[DM-ONLY]`

When the party shows him the bootprints or the oil-rag, Arlen goes still. Then he lies badly.

> *"Surveyors. The Exchange licenses surveyors. It's — it's ordinary. I'll note it."*

**DC 12 Insight:** he is frightened, and not of the ghoul. He does not confess tonight. That is Session 3's Clue C.

**Do not** have him volunteer the truth. Let the players suspect him.

### 4.3 Helena, if they call up the shaft

If anyone climbs back up to report, Helena listens to the bootprint description and her face closes.

> *"Rorik's crew. Had to be. They've been sniffing at Nine for a month."* (beat) *"I don't like being second to a vault I hold the writ for."*

She will not pull them out. She will offer **10 gp each** to keep going and bring her proof. **Tick Cartel Interference [X][ ][ ]** the first time the party says the word "Cartel" in her hearing — word travels.

### 4.4 The dying Breaker `[recommended]`

If stabilized, **Tamsin Roke** wakes in pieces. He is a Breaker, mid-twenties, frightened, and not loyal enough to die for Rorik.

> *"We came down three weeks back. Six of us. The book room was already open — we didn't open it. We just... walked in."*
> *"The chewer got Oll. I heard it for two days."*
> *"Rorik pays triple for bound books. No questions. But there's a gold man — Ilya coin — pays triple-triple for star-glass and pipe-metal. Rorik doesn't even ask what it's for."*

Then, if pressed or if he's fading:

> *"Down by the Reefs, at low tide — glass-faced ghosts. Their shadows don't follow. I saw one. I *saw* one."*

**This is the Soul Crisis rumour `[PLAYER-FACING]`.** It has **no mechanics**. Do not explain it. Do not tick the Soul Tide.

**His fate is the players' choice:** carry him up (slow, ticks the charter clock), leave him with a healer's kit (a coin-flip), or finish him. If they kill him, tick **Cartel Interference** and have Helena find out. If they save him, he becomes a recurring informant in Session 3 and beyond.

## 5. The Feast — Ghoul and Skeletons (60 min)

Run this as a **three-stage set-piece**, not a flat brawl.

### Stage 1 — The ghoul (rounds 1–3)

The ghoul is **gorging**. It has **no paralysis on its first round** and starts **prone** beside the body.

- If the party planned from the doorway: they get a surprise round. Reward it.
- If they walked in at normal speed: the ghoul rises and shrieks — which triggers Stage 2.

**Tactics:** it goes for the closest living target, tries to drag a downed PC back toward its meal (a **grapple**, not a coup de grâce), and flees toward the far dark at **5 HP or fewer**. It does not fight to the death — it is a scavenger.

### Stage 2 — The skeletons (round 2)

The shriek wakes **two skeletons** seated at the lecterns. They stand up **slowly**, shedding dust, and move to cut off the party's line back to the shaft.

> *They are still trying to file. One carries a stone tablet under its arm. It will not put it down, even to fight.*

**They are non-magical** — reassembled archive servitors, bone and old brass wire. No necromancer. When destroyed, the wire springs out and the bones collapse in a neat pile.

### Stage 3 — The mezzanine (round 3+)

If the fight is going badly for the party, the mezzanine gives them an out: **DC 13 Athletics** to haul someone onto the ledge, where the skeletons cannot climb (they have no climb speed and will stand below, reaching). If the fight is going well, the ghoul tries to escape up the spiral stair and the party can choose to let it go — **it will be back in Session 3**, wounded and hungry.

### Terrain

- **Lecterns:** half cover (+2 AC) for anyone crouching behind one.
- **Dust:** a 10-ft cloud wherever a fight happens. **Heavily obscured** until the end of the next round. Ranged attacks into it have disadvantage.
- **The sump:** a 5-ft square hole. **DC 10 Dex** to avoid on a charge; falling in costs 10 ft of movement and a scrape.
- **The mezzanine ledge:** 15 ft up. High ground, but no rail.

### Morale and mercy

- **The ghoul drags, it does not execute.** A downed PC is carried, not killed outright. That buys the party a rescue round.
- **The skeletons target the nearest standing PC** and never pursue past the doorway.
- **If two PCs are down**, Arlen screams and throws a lamp at the ghoul — a **1d4 fire** distraction that costs him his only useful action and gives the party one round. He is not a fighter; this should feel like a big deal.

### Scaling

| Table | Adjustment |
|-------|------------|
| 3 PCs | One skeleton only |
| 4 PCs | As written |
| 5+ PCs | Add a third skeleton, or give the ghoul its full 22 HP and paralysis from round 1 |
| Party at full resources and rolling hot | The ghoul flees at 5 HP and the skeletons are joined by a **Swarm of Rats** drawn by the blood |

### Aftermath

- The ghoul wears a **split bronze coin on a cord** — the Cartel mark. **Vector C.**
- The dead Breaker (Oll) has a purse with **12 gp**, a bone dice set, and a letter from a sister in New Ilya. Let them find the letter. It makes the body a person.
- The room is now a mess of dust and blood. **Tick Cartel Interference [X][ ][ ]** if they leave the bodies in the open; if they bury them under the lecterns, no tick, but it costs an hour.

## 6. Aftermath — the Ledger Leaf (30 min)

### 6.1 Finding it

The leaf is **not** on a lectern. It is in the ghoul's nest — a petrified sheet the ghoul dragged in with its meal, cracked at one edge.

> *A single leaf of stone-grey parchment, thin as a knife. The script on it is small, dense, and absolutely orderly. Columns. Totals. A date in the old reckoning.*

**DC 10 Investigation** to spot it in the nest, or automatic if they search the camp thoroughly.

### 6.2 Arlen translates

He needs **10 minutes** and reasonable light. He reads it twice before he says anything.

> *"Ward Three — fever beds: forty. Tithe: none. Oath-bound."*

Then, quieter:

> *"That's a hospital budget. Grain in, grain out. They were counting *sick people*, not sacrifices."*

This is **Clue B** and **Mystery 1, Stage 2** — `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`. The Ascensionists were civic administrators. Say it plainly, once, and let it land.

**If the players ask the obvious question** — *"then why does everyone say they were a death cult?"* — Arlen has no answer. Helena, if present, says: *"Because someone wrote the other version first."* Do not elaborate. That is Act II.

### 6.3 Optional: the catalog rod and the empty niches

If they found the bronze rod (§3.2 E), Arlen can match its graduations to the index wall. **DC 13 Investigation:** the empty niches are all in the same range — **catalog numbers 1,100 to 1,400**. The room's surviving records stop just before the Silence.

> *"That's not rot. That's a *gap*."*

**Do not** connect it to the Ascendants' pamphlet yet. Let the players do it.

## 7. Surface Breather (50 min)

They climb out. This is the session's second half, and it is **player-driven**: haul, talk, shop, rest, snoop.

### 7.1 The haul

They bring up:

- **Bronze fittings and catalog hardware** — 25 gp certified salvage (a DM addition; the big 120 gp cache is in 1.3).
- The **catalog rod**, if they took it — 15 gp, or keep it.
- The **ledger leaf** — an antiquity. **Not covered by the writ.** This matters in §7.3.
- The **split-coin token** — evidence, or 2 gp to the wrong buyer.

Helena records every item in her notebook, including the leaf, and does not touch it.

> *"That's writing. That's not mine. That's the Admiralty's, and you know it."* (beat) *"I'll pretend I didn't see it until you decide what you're doing."*

### 7.2 Helena's debrief

She is pleased about the salvage and **not** pleased about the bootprints. Play her tired, not angry.

- She confirms the coin is Rorik's mark.
- She will **not** go to the Admiralty yet — a formal complaint means an inspection, and an inspection means her warehouse is sealed.
- She offers **10 gp each** to finish the survey and **20 gp each** if they bring her proof of who else has been down there.
- **If they mention Tamsin Roke**, she goes very quiet. *"Rorik's man. Alive?"* She will pay for a healer, and she will want to talk to him herself. That is a thread for Session 3.

**Tick Helena's Charter** if they take a long rest before going back down. They have a day left on the writ.

### 7.3 The Antiquities Exchange (optional, recommended)

If they take the leaf to be certified, they walk into the campaign's central legal trap.

- The **Brass Astrolabe** is Arlen's workplace. His superior, **Sella Orr** `[NEW]`, is a tired, precise woman in an appraiser's apron who has seen too many young salvagers with a story.
- She will certify the **bronze** without blinking. She will *not* certify the **leaf** — and she will not report it, either. Not yet.
- **DC 13 Insight:** she is afraid of someone. She will not say who.
- **If they ask about the empty niches or the bootprints**, she says only: *"The Exchange doesn't pay for questions. It pays for provenance."*

**This scene is optional.** If the table is tired, skip it and move to §7.4.

### 7.4 Shopping and downtime

They now have roughly **25–40 gp each** (advance + haul share + Helena's bonus). Let them spend it.

**What's worth buying now:**

| Item | Price | Why |
|------|-------|-----|
| Healer's kit | 5 gp | The single best purchase at this level |
| Potion of healing | 50 gp | Still a stretch — one party pool purchase is a big moment |
| Climber's kit | 25 gp | The descent is now a known hazard |
| Crowbar | 2 gp | The brass door is next |
| Muffled picks + pitons | 25 gp | Advantage on Stealth; the Cartel uses these |
| Holy water | 25 gp | If they're spooked by the ghoul |
| Lantern + 10 oil | 6 gp | Dan's darkvision is not infinite |
| Chain shirt (if unarmored) | 50 gp | Probably next session |

**Downtime menu (pick one per PC, 10 minutes each):**

| Activity | What it does |
|----------|--------------|
| **Carouse** | DC 12 Persuasion/Performance. On a success, learn one dockside rumour (roll 1d6 on the Port Meridian table). On a failure, wake up with a hangover and a 5 gp tab |
| **Research** | DC 13 Investigation/History at the Astrolabe or a parish library. Learn one true thing about the old city — e.g. that the pre-Silence city had a *civic* hospital system |
| **Gossip** | DC 12 Insight with dockhands. Learn that Rorik's crew has been buying New Ilya oil by the crate |
| **Worship / parish** | Dan's home ground. Sister Marda's line about the dead not passing; no mechanics |
| **Craft** | Tinker's/Smith's tools. Repair gear, make 5 gp of trade goods, or improve one item's appearance |
| **Snoop on the Breakers** | DC 14 Stealth/Investigation. Find their flophouse near the Exchange; learn the name *Rorik* and that they answer to a "gold man" |

**Do not** let downtime eat the night. One scene per player, max, and cut between them.

### 7.5 The ward (if they saved Tamsin)

If Tamsin is alive and they took him to a Myrraenic ward (Sister Marda's, if Dan's player invokes her), he is sedated and talking in his sleep.

> *"The door was already open. The door was already open. We didn't open it."*

He will repeat that until he wakes. **No further information tonight.**

Instead, have a **single Breaker** watching from the far end of the warehouse. If spotted, he runs. If chased, he drops a survey chit and escapes. He is not a fight; he is a warning.

## 9. The Brass Door (10 min)

**Read-aloud:**

> *The far door is round, brass, and green with age. It sits a hand's width open. The air coming through it is dry and cold and smells of ink. The hiss is not steam. It is breath.*

**What they can learn without opening it:**

- **DC 12 Investigation:** the door is a **pneumatic** mechanism — a pressure seal, not a lock. The hiss is equalising.
- **DC 13 Investigation:** a **snapped steel pick** is jammed in the seam. Modern. The same make as the one in the dust.
- **DC 14 Perception:** beyond the door, something is **very still**. Not waiting. *Listening.*
- **Arlen:** *"That's a reading room. That's a *sealed* reading room. It should be dust and nothing else."*

**Do not open it.** If a player pushes, the door gives another inch, the hiss deepens — and the session ends on that. *"We'll open it next time."*
