## 3. Run Sheet

### Beat 0 — Open at the Table (10 min)

**Do not script the PCs' morning.** No dream vignette, no assigned parish, no invented debts. Those are the players' to invent.

**Set the room and hand them the floor:**

> *Third bell after dark. Rain like thrown gravel. The Salt-Lantern is loud in the way a room is loud when everyone in it is pretending not to look at the same table. Brine, wet wool, and lamp oil.*

Then, around the table:

- *"Where were each of you when the rain started, and what made you come here tonight?"*
- *"You've all heard Helena Voss is hiring. What do you need this money for?"*

One or two sentences each is plenty. Write down what they say.

**If a player has no answer ready**, do not fill it in for them: *"You're here because the job pays. We'll find out the rest in play."* Then move on.

**Dan's dream — optional, and the player's call.** Dan's backstory establishes that they dream and read it as Selyra's signs. If the player wants to open with that, ask: *"What did you dream last night?"* Let them narrate. If they'd rather not, skip it — nothing tonight depends on it. If they do describe rings, stairs, or water, confirm nothing. Note it and say nothing.

**Converge:** Helena's table. She is already seated and has been waiting long enough to be annoyed about it.

### Beat 1 — The Salt-Lantern Hire (50 min)

**Read-aloud:**

> *The Salt-Lantern smells of brine, wet wool, and the one honest thing in Port Meridian: lamp oil. A tiefling with a privateer's shoulders is drying glasses behind the bar. In the corner, three men in oilskins are not drinking. Everyone else is.*

**Staging**
- **Bess** behind the bar. Waters the ale, never the oil.
- **Helena Voss** at a window table with a leather notebook, a brass spyglass, and a pot of tea she is not drinking.
- **Arlen Marr** half a step behind her, ink on his fingers, a satchel of rubbing paper he keeps touching.
- **The Breakers** in the corner: three men in oilskins, not drinking, one with a split-coin token on a cord.

**The Breakers are strangers by default.** If a player says their PC knows one of them, take it and use it — but do not invent a shared history. If someone has a reason to recognise one, name him **Ospen Tarr** then. If nobody does, they stay three anonymous men who leave early and pay in Ilya coin.

**Helena's pitch (paraphrase, don't monologue):**
- Three-day private charter. Clear vermin, shore timber, map a collapsed fault under **Warehouse Nine**.
- **15 gp each + 10% of certified scrap**, paid whether they find anything or not.
- The writ covers **salvage**: rope, copper, worked stone. *"If it has writing on it, it isn't ours. It's the Admiralty's, which means it's nobody's, which means it's Rorik's by Tuesday."*
- Arlen comes along as recorder. He translates Old Elonian at half speed and cannot fight.
- **She wants to descend at first light.** Tonight is theirs. (This is the shopping window.)

**Negotiation levers (pick 2–3, don't offer all)**

These are things *any* PC can try. Do not reserve a lever for a named character.

| Lever | Check | Result |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Read the filed map | DC 12 Investigation (auto with Tinker's or cartographer's tools) | The fault runs under the neighbouring lot. Helena is impressed; +5 gp advance each |
| Push for hazard pay | DC 12 Persuasion or Insight | She admits the fault is bigger than filed; +5 gp advance each |
| Ask Bess for kit | DC 10 Persuasion, or free if the PC has a history with her | Bess lends a **climber's kit** and a **bullseye lantern** (advantage on the descent hazard) |
| Make a show of faith or ritual | Roleplay, no check | Flavour. Helena writes it down. Arlen asks a follow-up question and regrets it |
| Threaten to walk | DC 13 Intimidation | She withdraws the offer. Bess intervenes. Don't let this be the first scene's failure state |

**Helena interviews each PC.** One question per person, written down verbatim. Pick based on what the player has actually told you — never assign a backstory to justify a question:

- *"Can you work in the dark without a lamp?"* (she means noise, not faith)
- *"Can you read a manifest and tell me what's missing?"*
- *"Can I put you in front of a customs officer for ten minutes without it becoming a story?"*
- *"Can you hold a line without killing anyone?"*
- *"What's the worst thing you've brought up out of the dark?"*

**The Breakers:** they do not approach. One pays Bess in **Ilya coin** and leaves a crate of lamp oil on the dock. **Plant this. It pays off in Session 3.** If a PC talks to them, they are polite, evasive, and gone.

**Sign the writ.** Hand them the charter (end of file). Helena pays the advance in Coast sails — **15 gp each** (or 20 if they negotiated). Arlen shakes everyone's hand twice.

### Beat 3 — Warehouse Nine (20 min)

**Read-aloud:**

> *First light is a grey suggestion. The Warrens is a forest of bonded warehouses, iron shutters, and crane-rigs standing over the street like gallows. Somewhere a dog is barking at nothing. Somewhere closer, a chain is moving.*

**Warehouse Nine.** Cavernous. Hemp rope, timber crates, iron spikes, the smell of tar and old dust. **Foreman Cobb** — one arm, no intention of going below.

> "Cellar's all yours. I don't go past the third step. Not since the third step."
> "Rats chewed the rope. They always chew the rope. Buy your own rope."

**The cellar.** Salt-stained brick. A collapsed section of flagstones, four feet across, breathing cold air that smells of **wet lime and old paper**. A winch. Crates stamped HELENA VOSS / SALVAGE / NOT FOR RESALE.

**The clue in the cellar (plant it quietly):**
- A rope is already tied off around a roof beam. Old, frayed, knotted in a style Helena does not use.
- **DC 12 Investigation**, or automatic for anyone proficient with Tinker's Tools or sailor's knots: the knot is **modern**, and **not more than a month old**.
- Helena: *"That's not my rope. That's not my knot."* (beat) *"Write that down, Arlen."*

She will not speculate. She is a professional. Let the players do it.

**Do not** reveal the Cartels yet. The Breakers + the rope + the Ilya oil is enough.

### Beat 4 — Cellar Rats (25 min)

**Trigger:** the rats are already gnawing the coil of hemp rope the party needs. They scatter when the party enters, then come back.

**Encounter:** 3× **Giant Rat** (§9). Tutorial fight — teach turn order, cover, and the cost of noise.

**Twist:** the rope is the real stakes. If the party kills the rats loudly (shouting, *Thunderwave*, fire), the rope is further chewed: the descent hazard DC rises from 12 to 14. Quiet kill or driving them off: the rope holds. **Tell them this after the fight, not before.** Anyone with Tinker's Tools or rope can splice it (DC 12) to restore it.

**Noise:** if the fight is loud, tick **Helena's Charter [X][ ][ ][ ]** — a customs skiff passes the wharf and slows. Nobody comes in. Yet.

**Aftermath:** the cellar hatch is open to the street. If they leave it open, tick **Cartel Interference [X][ ][ ]**.

### Beat 5 — The Catalogue Descent (45 min)

**Read-aloud:**

> *Rope creaks above. Broken steps fall eighty feet into dark that smells of wet lime and old paper. The shaft is not brick all the way down. About halfway, the wall changes.*

**The descent (three pitches)**
1. **0–40 ft:** warehouse brick, wet, easy.
2. **40–60 ft:** the wall becomes **poured stone with no seams**. Anyone with masonry, Tinker's Tools, or a relevant background: not dwarf-work or modern guild-work. The tolerance is too fine. *"Someone machined this."*
3. **60–80 ft:** a frieze, half-lit by the party's lantern. Then the floor.

**Hazard — loose scree.** DC 12 Dex save or slide 20 ft and take 1d6 bludgeoning. **Pitons + DC 10 Athletics negates.** Climber's kit: advantage. Chewed rope: DC 14.

**Clue A — the bas-relief `[ESTABLISHED CANON — Mystery 1, Stage 1]`**

> *Robed figures in a frieze. One holds a sickle and a sheaf of grain. One holds scales. One holds a compass and a scroll. One is holding a child's hand. There is a bed with a figure lying on it, and a channel of water running to a field. No skulls. No altars. No knives.*

- **DC 11 History or Religion:** **civic iconography**, not cult. A municipal seal. A city doing paperwork.
- **Arlen** (no check): *"That's not a crypt. That's an office. That's a very old, very serious office."* He tries to take a rubbing. Helena tells him to wait.
- **If a PC is a cleric or religious:** the figure with the compass has a crescent over one shoulder — Selyra, or a pre-Silence artist's idea of her. Recognisable from a hymn, not a lecture.
- **If a PC is an artificer, smith, or mason:** the compass is a measuring instrument, and the stone is cut to a tolerance that should not exist in a basement.
- **DC 13 Investigation, anyone looking closely:** the faces are worn smooth — not by water. Rubbed. Deliberately. **Say nothing more.**

**The shoring.** The timber shoring at the bottom is unsafe. **DC 12 with Tinker's Tools, mason's tools, or a crowbar and a good argument** shores it, making the passage safe for Arlen and Helena. If they do it, Helena quietly adds that PC's name to the charter as **second surveyor** (+10 gp at the end). If they don't, Arlen comes down anyway and is a liability in the next fight.

### Beat 6 — 1.1 Centipedes (40 min)

**Trigger:** the shoring moves.

**Read-aloud:**

> *The timber shoring creaks — once, twice — and then the sound resolves into something else. A dry, fast ticking, like a hundred fingernails on a table.*

**Warning:** **DC 13 Perception**. Passive Perception 13+ notices automatically. If warned, the party gets one round to prepare (light a torch, form up, or climb around the nest with DC 13 Acrobatics). If not, the centipedes move first.

**Encounter:** 3× **Giant Centipede** (§9), arriving 2 on round 1 and 1 on round 2.

**Tactics:** nearest warm body, climb walls and ceiling, flee open flame. Vermin, not tacticians. One goes for the lantern oil — if it spills, they lose light for the rest of the descent.

**Terrain:** a 10-ft band of scree is difficult terrain. Fire or thunder here: **DC 12 Dex** or 1d6 from falling grit.

**Scaling**
- **3 players, or a bad start:** 2 centipedes.
- **Steamrolling:** a **Swarm of Rats** pours out of the nest on round 3.
- **A PC drops:** the centipedes lose interest and go for the oil. Two rounds to stabilise. Do not let a tutorial fight become a death spiral.

## 10. Optional DM Levers (decide before play)

All optional. All planted without explanation. None requires a PC to have a particular backstory.

- **The maker's plate.** If you want a long fuse to the campaign's patron, a brass plate can be found on a construct PC's chassis or inside salvaged frame, stamped **KESSLER & VANE — SALVAGE WORKS**. Do not mention it. Let them find it in Act II or III. **Only with the player's buy-in** — it edits their character's history.
- **The calibration disc.** In the scree, a palm-sized brass disc with concentric rings, stamped with a tiny falcon-and-calipers. Worthless to Helena (not on the manifest). Priceless in Act IV. Whoever picks it up keeps it. **Do not explain it.**
- **The rubbed faces.** Leave unresolved. Water, vandalism, or censorship. Act II answers it.
- **The prophet.** He says something too close to home for whichever PC engages him, and leaves.
- **The dream.** Dan's player may open with a dream. Do not supply one, and do not confirm it means anything.

## 11. If / Then

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| **Party refuses the job** | Helena shrugs and hires the Breakers. Bess mentions that Rorik's crew will strip the vault and sell the writing by the pound. One night to change their minds; if not, they can follow the Breakers down as freelancers. Do not force the charter. |
| **Party spends the whole night shopping** | Fine. That is the beat. Cut the street texture and shorten the rats. The descent still fits. |
| **Party skips shopping entirely** | Fine. They descend under-equipped. Let the missing healer's kit and pitons matter, without gloating. |
| **Party tries to keep the frieze a secret** | Fine. Arlen takes a rubbing anyway. The Exchange finds out in Session 3. |
| **Party attacks the Breakers in the tavern** | Bess throws them out. The Breakers leave. Tick Cartel Interference **twice**. Helena withdraws the offer unless they fix it. A legitimate path — let it cost them. |
| **Party goes loud in the cellar** | Tick the charter clock. A customs skiff investigates in 10 minutes. Hide, bribe (10 gp), or talk their way out. |
| **Someone drops to 0 HP** | Centipedes lose interest. *Spare the Dying*, a healer's kit, or *Cure Wounds* stabilises. If nobody can, Arlen panics and Helena arrives with a kit — furious, and the clock ticks. |
| **They solve the descent trivially** | Reward it. Spend the saved time on Arlen and Helena at the bottom, and let the frieze breathe. |
| **They run long** | Cut the street texture and the cellar rats. Go straight from the hire to the descent. |
| **They run short** | **Do not start 1.2.** Add: Helena and Arlen come down, Arlen takes his rubbing, Helena interviews the party about what they saw. End on the furrow anyway. |
| **They try to push into 1.2** | The doorway is the edge of the map tonight. Give them the furrow, the sound, and the dust — then say plainly, *"We'll pick this up next time."* |

## 12. Closing the Night

**Recap (read or paraphrase):**
> *You signed a charter for three days' pay. You walked a city that sells its own past by the ounce. You went down eighty feet into a dark that smelled of paper. You found a frieze of farmers, physicians, and surveyors — not a death cult. You found a rope that wasn't Helena's. And at the bottom, in two feet of dust, something had already walked.*

**Ask the table, not each PC by name:**
- *"What does your character think is down there?"*
- *"Who in this crew do you trust least, and why?"*
- *"What's the one thing you didn't tell Helena?"*

**Between sessions:** collect the §1 answers. If Kit's player hasn't chosen a patron, leave it open. Do not write their backstory for them.

**Milestone:** no level-up tonight. Level 2 lands after the **Witness Scriptorium** (Session 3).

## 13. Handout — The Charter Writ

> **CHARTER OF PRIVATE SALVAGE — No. 9 / 1,000 AS**
> **Grantor:** Helena Voss, Licensed Factor, Free Coast
> **Holder(s):** the undersigned crew
> **Term:** three days from signing
> **Site:** Warehouse Nine, Salvage Warrens, Port Meridian — cellar fault and associated underworks
>
> **1.** The holder shall clear vermin, shore timber, and map the collapsed fault to the depth marked on the filed plan.
> **2.** **Salvage** — rope, copper, worked stone, and unmixed scrap — is the property of the grantor, to be certified at the Antiquities Exchange and divided per the agreed tenth-share.
> **3.** **Antiquities** — any object bearing writing, inscription, seal, image of worship, or star-chart — are **not** covered by this writ. They are the property of the Admiralty Board and must be declared at the Gilded Docks.
> **4.** Concealment of antiquities is a fine of **500 gp** or **impressment** into the Admiralty fleet, at the Board's discretion.
> **5.** The grantor assumes no liability for death, dismemberment, or undeath.
>
> *Signed: ______________  Witness: Arlen Marr, Junior Archivist*

**DM note:** clause 3 is the whole campaign in miniature. Read it aloud. Let the players hear the trap before they walk into it.
