# Faction Dossier: The Archive Cartels

> *"Scholars argue over who owns history; kings argue over who writes it. We sell it to the highest bidder by the pound."*  
> **Master Faction Profile • The Antiquities Black Market & Salvage Syndicates**

---

## 1. Executive Summary `[PLAYER-FACING]`

- **Classification:** Transnational Criminal Syndicate, Black-Market Antiquities Cartel & Salvage Guild.
- **Founding Era:** c. 400 AS (Formed during the economic rise of the Free Coast).
- **Headquarters:** Secret auction vaults beneath the **Antiquities Exchange** in Port Meridian, with covert branches in New Ilya and Solcaris Nova.
- **Supreme Factor:** **Vance Rorik** ("The Ledger Master").
- **Core Principle:** **Knowledge and Relics Possess Material Market Value.**
- **Symbol:** A split bronze coin over an open lockpick.
- **Public Perception:** High-end antiquities dealers, salvage brokers, and private appraisal houses. Beneath this legitimate corporate veneer lies a cutthroat criminal underworld of tomb-raiders, smugglers, fence-houses, and assassin cells.

---

## 2. Campaign Function: The Unscrupulous Competitors `[DM-ONLY]`

`[ESTABLISHED CANON]`
The Archive Cartels serve three distinct narrative and tactical roles across the campaign:
1. **The Rivals of Act I:** In Port Meridian, the Cartels operate competing salvage teams racing Helena Voss and the party to loot the Shattered Archive. They have no interest in historical preservation; they rip illuminated pages out of codices to sell to private collectors.
2. **The Unwitting Accomplices of House Vane:** Lord Seraphel’s agents use the Cartels to acquire rare Pre-Silence alloy pipes, star-glass lenses, and ancient seals. The Cartels sell to House Vane enthusiastically because Seraphel pays triple market rates in pure gold, completely indifferent to what he uses the artifacts for.
3. **The Black-Market Pipeline:** If the party misses crucial clues or ancient keys in ruins, the Cartels provide a natural fallback vector: the missing leaf or token appears on the black auction block in Port Meridian or New Ilya the following week.

## 4. Faction Resources & Table Encounters

### Black-Market Goods Available (For Coin)
- **Stolen Primary Texts:** Fragmented pages from redacted libraries (100–500 gp per page).
- **Infiltration Equipment:** Alchemical acid vials for melting Pre-Silence bronze locks, silence-scrolls, and muffled mining picks.
- **Illicit Relic Fencing:** Willingness to buy forbidden artifacts that university deans or League magistrates would immediately confiscate.

### Faction NPC Archetype: Cartel Enforcer / Salvage Captain (CR 4)
*Medium Humanoid, Neutral Evil*  
**AC:** 15 (Studded Leather) • **HP:** 60 (11d8 + 11) • **Speed:** 30 ft.  
**STR:** 14 (+2) | **DEX:** 16 (+3) | **CON:** 12 (+1) | **INT:** 12 (+1) | **WIS:** 10 (+0) | **CHA:** 14 (+2)  
**Skills:** Athletics +4, Deception +4, Intimidation +4, Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +5  
**Dirty Fighting:** When hitting an enemy with a melee weapon, the enforcer can force the target to make a DC 13 Con save or be blinded until the end of its next turn (pocket sand or pommel strike).  
**Actions — Multiattack:** Makes two Scimitar strikes and one Dagger strike.  
**Scimitar:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage.
