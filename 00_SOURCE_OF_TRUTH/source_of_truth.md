## How to use this folder

1. Read the **Bible** first. It is short and absolute.
2. Read **Ain Elonar** as the story the world tells about itself — not as a neutral history lecture.
3. When a downstream file disagrees, the source of truth wins.
4. Never invent canonical answers for the protected unknowns: the Sea Beyond, human origins, the ultimate destination of souls, whether Myrraen was right, or the blank final chapter of *Ain Elonar*.
