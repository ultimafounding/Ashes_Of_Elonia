# Frontier and Ruin Sites: The Lost Wonders of Elonia

> *"Between the five nations lies an unmapped world of shattered marble, subterranean glass highways, and cities where the silence has lasted a thousand years."*  
> **Location Architecture • Frontier Wilderness & Ruin Gazetteers**

---

## 1. Overview `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`

Beyond the populated urban hubs and sovereign capitals of Elonia lie the physical remnants of the **Age of Revelation** and the **Waning Age**. These sites are not merely inert stone piles—they are active metaphysical conduits, hazard zones, and geopolitical flashpoints contested by archaeologists, salvage cartels, and covert factions.

---

## 2. Keyed Frontier & Ruin Gazetteers

---

### 1. The Ruins of Ancient Solcaris & The Resonant Pylons
- **Region:** The Sun-Plains (Border between Aurelian League and Eastern Wastes).
- **Historic Patron:** Aurelion, the Sovereign Flame.
- **Tone & Landscape:** Blinding white marble, melted gold domes, scorched basalt pavements, and absolute solar radiation. Built beside the dried bed of the **Sea of Glass**.
- **Key Features:**
  - **The Shattered Sun-Dome:** A fallen dome four hundred feet across, radiating ambient radiant heat. Mundane metals left here glow red-hot within minutes.
  - **The Resonant Pylons:** Twelve colossal obelisks of golden alloy arranged in a mathematical circle. Built during the First Covenant to stabilize continental ley-lines.
- **The Act V Threat `[DM-ONLY]`:** In Act V, Lord Seraphel’s vanguard seizes the Pylons, installing siphon-nodes to invert their solar resonance into soul-funnels feeding the Engine Deep.

---

### 2. Harad Veyr & The Spine of Thaeron
- **Region:** The Western Mountain Crags (The Iron Peaks).
- **Historic Patron:** Thaeron, the Iron Heart.
- **Tone & Landscape:** Brutal, vertical, wind-sheer, and industrial. Colossal fortress-cities chiseled directly into black granite cliffs, connected by massive suspension bridges of cold iron chains.
- **Key Features:**
  - **The Great Anvil of Harad:** A four-story iron monolith where weapons for the defense of mortals were once forged. Strikes against the anvil still ring across the valleys like thunder.
  - **The Chasm of the Undaunted:** A bottomless crevasse across which ancient defenders held the pass during the Fall of the Nine Cities.
- **Hazards:** Gale-force blizzards, crumbling iron cat-walks, and **Forge-Golems** that continue to patrol the foundry levels, attacking anyone lacking ancient military badges.

---

### 3. The Star Roads Network (The Subterranean Arteries)
- **Region:** Subterranean Elonia (Spanning from Port Meridian to Nythara and the Engine Deep).
- **Historic Architects:** Orinth, Selyra, and the ancient mortal engineers.
- **Tone & Landscape:** Smooth, subterranean tubes bored through solid bedrock, paved with iridescent, luminous **Star-Glass**. Completely silent, sterile, and free of dust.
- **Key Features:**
  - **The Waystations:** Domed chambers situated every fifty miles containing dormant water cisterns, glowing astronomical dials, and brass arrival bells.
  - **The Velocity Cars:** Levitating brass skiffs propelled by magnetic ley-lines. Capable of traveling 100 miles per hour when powered by a *Shard of Nythara* or *Covenant Seal*.
- **The Hazard `[DM-ONLY]`:** Large sections of the Star Roads are collapsed, flooded, or breached by the soulways, creating sections where cars crash into pockets of screaming, unpassed spirits.

---

### 4. The Hollow Glades of The Verdancy
- **Region:** Deep Interior Rainforest of The Verdancy.
- **Historic Patron:** Elyndra, the Verdant Queen.
- **Tone & Landscape:** Primeval, suffocatingly lush, and bioluminescent. Trees whose trunks measure two hundred feet in diameter support entire arboreal ecosystems.
- **Key Features:**
  - **The Root-Labyrinth:** An underground network of living ironwood roots extending for hundreds of miles, used by Canopy Rangers for covert travel.
  - **The Grove of the First Sapling:** An ancient clearing where plants exhibit miraculous growth rates. Wounds bound with leaves from this grove heal overnight.
- **Hazards:** Spore-clouds inducing hallucinatory trance-states, predatory carnivorous flora (Corpse-Orchids), and territorial beast-tribes enforcing the Iron Taboo.

---

### 5. The Shattered Reefs of the Meridian Gulf
- **Region:** Off the southern coast of the Free Coast.
- **Historic Patron:** Kaelis, the Wandering Star.
- **Tone & Landscape:** Jagged sea-stacks, turquoise tidal lagoons, and submerged marble arches. Pre-Silence aqueducts and harbor-towers rise from the ocean like broken ribcages.
- **Key Features:**
  - **The Drowned Scriptorium:** A submerged archive accessible only at dead low tide or via water-breathing magic, containing navigational maps of the outer seas.
  - **The Siren Spire:** An ancient lighthouse whose magical crystal prism still turns automatically, casting beam-arcs that reveal invisible creatures across three miles of open water.
- **Hazards:** Riptides, kraken-spawn, reef sharks, and sunken galleons crewed by drowned sailors bound to the unpassed soulways.

---

### 6. The Ashen Crater of Avarr
- **Region:** The Dead Basin (Central Continental Wasteland).
- **Historic Status:** The former *Jewel of the World*, destroyed following the crisis of certainty (c. 1,600 BS).
- **Tone & Landscape:** A colossal, thirty-mile crater of vitrified black glass and pale white ash. Nothing grows here; the air tastes of sulfur and copper.
- **Key Features:**
  - **The Glass Spires:** Slender towers of melted obsidian sticking up from the ash-drifts like blackened needles.
  - **The Plaza of the Inquest:** The central forum where the Celestine descended to shatter Avarr’s leadership.
- **Metaphysical Reality `[DM-ONLY]`:** The crater is the single strongest locus of **Memory Storms** in Elonia. Visitors are bombarded by psychic echoes of citizens singing hymns while burning their neighbors, reinforcing the horror of moral certainty without empathy.
