# Location Profile: Port Meridian & The Shattered Archive

> *"The docks smell of brine, pitch, and ambition. Mortals built this city on the bones of an age they barely remember, and they sell pieces of it by the ounce."*  
> **Campaign Hub: Act I (Levels 1–4) • The Free Coast**

---

## 1. City Overview `[PLAYER-FACING]`

- **Classification:** Sovereign Maritime Free City & Deepwater Trade Metropolis.
- **Population:** ~65,000 (Human 55%, Halfling 15%, Gnome 10%, Dwarf 8%, Tiefling 5%, Scaled-kin/Other 7%).
- **Governance:** The **Admiralty Board** and the **High Consortium of Harbor Guilds**.
- **Tone & Aesthetic:** Crowded, salty, vibrant, and morally pragmatic. Salt-encrusted timber wharves buttress monumental Pre-Silence sea-walls of dark cyclopean basalt. Skiffs, cog-ships, and sleek Free Coast privateers crowd the harbor beneath towering crane-rigs powered by waterwheels and gnomish counterweights.

## 2. Keyed City Districts

### 1. The Gilded Docks & Admiralty Wharf
- **Description:** The beating commercial heart of the Free Coast. A labyrinth of floating piers, drydocks, and customs checkpoints where galleons from the Aurelian League and river-barges from New Ilya unload grain, timber, wine, and contraband.
- **Key Location — The Salt-Lantern Tavern:** A boisterous taphouse frequented by salvagers, mapmakers, and sellswords. Run by **"Barnacle" Bess**, a retired tiefling privateer who knows every unpermitted expedition leaving the bay.
- **Atmosphere:** Screaming gulls, creaking hemp cordage, shouted sea-shanties, and the pungent reek of gutted cod and tar.

### 2. The Salvage Warrens & Warehouse Nine
- **Description:** A sprawling waterfront district of bonded warehouses, iron-foundries, and tenement blocks. Beneath the floorboards of these warehouses lie the sealed entries to Pre-Silence under-vaults.
- **Key Location — Warehouse Nine:** Owned by factor **Helena Voss**. A cavernous storage depot for hemp rope, timber crates, and iron spikes. Beneath its cellar floor lies a collapsed masonry fault leading directly into the **Shattered Archive**.

### 3. The Antiquities Exchange
- **Description:** A grand colonnaded plaza of auction houses, pawn-brokers, appraisal syndicates, and notary offices. Here, artifacts hauled from beneath the earth are legally certified—or illicitly fences away from municipal tax collectors.
- **Key Location — The Brass Astrolabe:** An elite appraisal house operated by junior archivist **Arlen Marr**, who secretly collaborates with independent Ascendants to authenticate pre-Silence manuscripts before the Cartels seize them.

### 4. The Lower Reefs (The Sunken Foundations)
- **Description:** At low tide, the jagged remnants of colossal Pre-Silence arches, aqueducts, and drowned plazas emerge from the foaming surf of Meridian Bay.
- **Hazards:** Submerged rip-tides, razor-sharp barnacle encrustations, and predatory reef-crawlers that nest within ancient drainage pipes.

### 5. The Admiralty Citadel
- **Description:** A fortress of black basalt perched atop the harbor's southern promontory. Houses the Admiralty Courts, the naval garrison, and the lockup cells where unlicensed tomb-robbers await forced naval impressment.

## 3. Subterranean Site: The Shattered Archive `[DM-ONLY]`

Located eighty feet beneath Warehouse Nine, the **Shattered Archive** is an ancient administrative scriptorium built by the early **Ascensionists** during the Waning Age (c. 1,100 BS).

```
   [Warehouse Nine Cellar]
             │
      (Rope Descent)
             ▼
   [1.1 Catalogue Descent] ──► [1.2 Hall of Records]
                                       │
   [1.3 Witness Scriptorium] ◄─────────┘
             │
   [1.4 Star Chamber] (Climactic Vault)
```

### Keyed Dungeon Chambers
- **1.1 The Catalogue Descent:** A steeply sloping stone corridor choked with fallen masonry and ancient water-damage. Feral tunnel-scourges (ghouls and giant centipedes) nest in the collapsed crawlspaces.
- **1.2 The Hall of Records:** Rows of stone lecterns covered in petrified parchment dust. Relief carvings on the walls depict mortal scholars with geometric tools measuring celestial orbits—not demon-worshipers.
- **1.3 The Witness Scriptorium:** A preserved reading room sealed by a damaged pneumatic brass door. Contains the *Oath of the Scriptorium* inscribed in Pre-Silence High Elonian, proving the Ascensionists sought mortal medical self-reliance.
- **1.4 The Star Chamber:** A vaulted dome decorated with inlaid lapis lazuli and silver filigree mimicking Selyra's constellations. In the center rests the stone plinth holding the **Catalogue Codex**.

### The Twist in the Dust `[DM-ONLY]`
Inside Chamber 1.3, an active investigation check (DC 13) reveals **fresh iron boot-heel marks, broken modern lockpicks, and a discarded grease-rag with the chemical scent of New Ilya lamp oil**. Someone modern has been surveying these vaults within the last month.

## 4. Local Factions in Port Meridian

- **The Free Ascendants (Arlen Marr's Circle):** Idealistic researchers seeking truth without imperial censorship.
- **The Archive Cartels:** Cutthroat black-market antiquities smugglers led by crime-boss **Vance Rorik**. They use sellswords to loot ruins and assassinate rival scholars.
- **House Vane Representative:** **Lord Seraphel** maintains a luxurious diplomatic townhouse on the harbor bluff, observing the salvage trade and discreetly scouting capable adventurers.
