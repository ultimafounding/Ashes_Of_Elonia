## Operational Rules for Location Design

1. **Physical Evidence Leads:** Every location profile provides tangible environmental clues (foundry stamps, margin notes in manuscripts, altered architecture) that reveal lore through investigation rather than passive NPC monologues.
2. **Keyed Spatial Geometries:** All major dungeon sites and urban districts include directional ASCII / Mermaid structural diagrams for clear table running.
3. **Random Encounter Tables (1d6 / 1d8):** Hubs and wilderness biomes feature balanced encounter tables that showcase social intrigue, faction skirmishes, and the escalating symptoms of the Soul Crisis.
4. **Canonical Guardrails:** Locations preserve the locked canon of `00_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH/` and protect setting unknowns.
