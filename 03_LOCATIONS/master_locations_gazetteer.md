## 3. DM Regional Running Notes

- **The River Ilya Route:** Traveling from Port Meridian to New Ilya is most safely accomplished via merchant packet-boat up the coast and into the River Ilya delta, reducing the journey from 20 overland days to **7 river-sailing days**.
- **The Weeping Ridge Barrier:** Crossing the Weeping Ridge without a native guide or the blessing of the Church of the Last Light incurs an extra 1d4 days lost in supernatural fog.
- **The Subterranean Shortcut:** Once the party secures the *Keys of Nythara* in Act V, the Star Roads unlock instantaneous transit across the continent, allowing them to rapidly strike Seraphel’s siphon-nodes before the Winter Solstice.
