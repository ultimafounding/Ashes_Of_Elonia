# Master Chronology: The History of Elonia

> *"In the naming of things, knowledge was born. In the silence of things, freedom was earned."*  
> **World Architecture • Definitive Timeline & Era Concordance**

---

## 1. Calendar Systems & Chronological Reconciliation

`[ESTABLISHED CANON]`
Elonian historiography is divided into two primary epochs demarcated by the **Silence**:
- **BS (Before the Silence):** The ancient mythic and divine eras of the First Covenant, the Nine Cities, and the Ascensionists.
- **AS (After the Silence):** The modern era of mortal reconstruction, secular nations, and the five great powers.

### Canonical Anchor Ruling
`[ESTABLISHED CANON]`
Earlier drafts and regional texts contain conflicting references between "c. 1,100 AS" and "exactly one thousand years after the Abandonment." **The Official Campaign Bible locks the Present Day at c. 1,000 AS.** All downstream records, campaign clocks, and character backgrounds are anchored to this date.

### Era I: The Mythic Era (Before Time – c. 3,500 BS)

#### Before Time: The Sea Beyond `[UNRESOLVED]`
- Before the naming of stars or the roots of mountains, there exists only the **Sea Beyond**—a vastness of unformed potential and possibility.
- The world of Elonia exists unshaped and waiting. It is not empty, but unawakened.

#### The Age of Dawn: The Awakening of the Nine `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`
- **Orinth** awakens first beneath an unlit sky. He names the stone, the wind, and the sea, giving birth to knowledge.
- **Selyra** opens her eyes and dreams silver fires adrift in the blackness; the myths hold her dreaming called the stars — whether truth or allegory, none can say.
- Orinth and Selyra wander together across uncounted centuries, seeking the horizon.
- The remaining Seven awaken: **Aurelion** rises amidst fire and storm; **Thaeron** awakens upon an unformed battlefield; **Elyndra** stirs beneath primordial roots; **Kaelis** stirs at the world’s furthest edge; **Iskara** awakens to the warmth of living breath.
- **Varek** comes in silence. With his arrival, the first leaf falls: endings and departure enter the world, and Varek takes up the maintenance of the passage of souls.
- **Myrraen** is the last to awaken, stepping into the world with tears of compassion for mortal fragility.

#### The Age of Wonder: The Coming of Mortals `[ESTABLISHED CANON]` `[UNRESOLVED]`
- The Celestine discover humans already wandering the river valleys of Elonia. Mortal origins remain `[UNRESOLVED]`.
- The gods are awed by human **mutability**—the uniquely mortal ability to change moral nature, break vows, contradict expectations, and risk ruin for ideals.

### Era II: The Divine Age & The Great Concord (c. 3,500 – 1,600 BS)

#### c. 3,500 BS: The First Covenant `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`
- Mortals and the Celestine forge the First Covenant. It is a partnership, not an empire of servitude.
- The Celestine offer knowledge, medicine, laws, and guidance; mortals offer reverence, companionship, and cultivation of the earth.

#### c. 3,200 – 2,000 BS: The Nine Cities and the Age of Revelation
- Nine magnificent city-civilizations flourish across Elonia, each reflecting a divine ideal:
  - **Solcaris:** Gleaming white stone beside the Sea of Glass, founded upon Aurelion's principle that law must be stronger than kings.
  - **Ilyanor:** The city of blue-tiled spires, founded upon Orinth's belief that knowledge is sacred because it can be questioned.
  - **Nythara:** The roofless mountain city of dark reflection pools, where Selyra's stargazers read possibility.
  - **Vale Myrr:** The wall-less sanctuary city in the broad river basin, embodying Myrraen’s faith in radical mercy.
  - **Harad Veyr:** Thaeron's fortress city where martial strength is taught as defensive responsibility.
  - **The Living Groves of Elyndra:** Vast settlements cultivated in balance with primordial ecosystems.
  - **Vareth-Tor:** The basalt necropolis of Varek, preserving the names and lineages of the dead.
  - **Iskar-Vane:** The city of bridges, music, and eternal covenants under Iskara.
  - **The Horizon Harbors of Kaelis:** Ports from which mortal explorers sail into the unknown.
- The **Star Roads** are laid down: a continent-spanning network of subterranean transit conduits paved with star-glass.

#### c. 2,000 – 1,600 BS: The Rise of Avarr, Jewel of the World
- Situated at the heart of trade routes, the empire of **Avarr** synthesizes the best virtues of all Nine Cities.
- For four centuries, Avarr is celebrated as the pinnacle of human achievement. Its literacy, hospitals, courts, and arts are unmatched.

---

### Era III: The Waning Age (c. 1,600 – 0 BS)

#### c. 1,600 BS: The Atrocities of Avarr & The Crisis of Certainty `[ESTABLISHED CANON]` `[DM-ONLY]`
- Avarran philosophers develop theories of civilizational stages. Convinced of their moral duty to elevate "lesser" peoples, Avarr's leadership enacts systematic cultural purges, forced displacement, and ideological executions.
- The perpetrators act not out of malice, but out of total conviction that they are serving the greater good.
- The Celestine intervene and dismantle Avarr's leadership.
- **The Epiphany of Doubt:** Myrraen is deeply shaken. She realizes that paradise, education, and divine protection did not prevent evil; instead, absolute certainty weaponized it.

#### 1,500 – 1,300 BS: The Century of Interventions
- As mortal confidence wavers, the Celestine intervene more frequently to halt famines, floods, and wars.
- A toxic dynamic forms: mortals grow dependent on divine rescue, losing the capacity to solve their own crises.

#### c. 1,250 BS: Selyra’s Nine-Day Vision
- Selyra retreats to the highest peak of Nythara and weeps for nine days. She observes branching futures and realizes that perpetual divine presence leads to total spiritual atrophy.

#### 1,200 – 1,050 BS: Rise of the Ancient Ascensionists `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`
- A mortal movement of physicians, engineers, and philosophers—the **Ascensionists**—argues that humanity must learn to survive without divine miracles.
- They develop secular medicine, advanced clockwork engineering, and non-divine arcana.

#### c. 1,000 BS: The Ascension Engine Project `[ESTABLISHED CANON]` `[DM-ONLY]`
- Seeking to overcome the ultimate vulnerability of mortality—death itself—the Ascensionists construct the titanic **Ascension Engine** deep beneath the central bedrock of Elonia.
- The Engine is designed to weave human souls into an eternal, self-sustaining loop.

#### 980 – 950 BS: The Catastrophe of the Engine & The Soulway Fracture
- The Ascension Engine overloads catastrophically. Millions of souls are shattered and trapped.
- The ancient **Soulways**—the metaphysical pathways of natural passage—sustain massive, permanent structural trauma. Varek intervenes to halt the overload, but the spiritual fabric of Elonia is permanently scarred.

#### 901 BS: The Last Council
- The Nine Celestine convene in agonizing debate over the fate of Elonia.
- Aurelion demands martial law; Thaeron demands mortals suffer their own consequences; Selyra warns of doom if they stay.
- Myrraen makes her fateful decision: divine protection has become a cage. To save humanity, the gods must leave.

### Era IV: The Silence (0 AS)

#### 0 AS: The Abandonment & The Erection of the Veil `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`
- Myrraen channels her immense divine authority and severs the celestial link to Elonia.
- The **Veil** is drawn across the cosmos. Eight of the Nine Celestine are drawn beyond.
- **Orinth’s Secret Defiance:** `[DM-ONLY]` Refusing to abandon humanity to despair, Orinth strips himself of his divine majesty and remains behind on Elonia in secret, taking the identity of humble **Brother Cael**.

---

### Era V: The World Without Gods (0 – 1,000 AS)

#### 0 – 100 AS: The Years of Ash & The Fall of the Nine Cities `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`
- With miracle-based agriculture and magical systems abruptly severed, continent-wide famine, disease, and resource wars erupt.
- Solcaris collapses; Ilyanor burns in riots; Harad Veyr is abandoned; Avarr's ruins become haunted wastelands.

#### 0 – 300 AS: The Age of Ashes
- Mortals endure brutal centuries of survival. Gradually, secular institutions, crop rotation, mortal medicine, and independent arcane traditions are born from necessity.

#### 300 – 650 AS: The Age of Nations & The Five Powers
- From the ashes of ancient realms, the modern world crystallizes into the **Five Great Powers**:
  - The *Aurelian League* unifies the solar plains.
  - The *Republic of Ilya* rebuilds around the Grand Lyceum.
  - The *Dominion of Vareth* restores funerary science.
  - *The Verdancy* establishes arboreal druidic confederacies.
  - *The Free Coast* transforms maritime trade through Port Meridian.

#### c. 650 AS: The Resurfacing Soul Crisis `[DM-ONLY]`
- The ancient wound inflicted by the Ascension Engine begins to manifest measurably.
- In Khar Vareth, scribes record a 15% to 25% failure rate in peaceful soul transit. Spirits linger as tortured phantoms.
- The Republic of Ilya and Aurelian League establish secret censorship units (including the *Athenaeum Null*) to suppress news of the failing soulways.

#### c. 850 AS: The Modern Ascendants Emerge `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`
- A new, pluralistic historical and archaeological movement arises, taking the ancient name **The Ascendants**. Unlike their ancient predecessors, modern Ascendants are historians, antiquarians, and scholars seeking recovered truth.

#### c. 970 AS: Seraphel Discovers the Wound `[DM-ONLY]`
- Lord Seraphel of House Vane discovers surviving subterranean conduits of the Ascension Engine.
- Realizing that millions of unpassed souls are trapped within the wound, Seraphel begins constructing covert brass siphon-arrays to harvest the spiritual power for his own planned apotheosis.

#### c. 1,000 AS: Present Day (The Convergence Horizon) `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`
- **The Campaign Begins:** The party takes on an excavation contract beneath Warehouse Nine in Port Meridian. The embers beneath the ashes begin to glow.
