## Canon Rules for World Content

1. **The Present Year is c. 1,000 AS:** The 1,100 AS date from draft manuscripts is superseded.
2. **The Ascendants vs. The Ascensionists:** Modern Ascendants are a pluralistic historical/archaeological movement; ancient Ascensionists were Pre-Silence reformers who built the Ascension Engine.
3. **The Soul Crisis:** The failure of souls to pass stems from the ancient physical/metaphysical damage inflicted by the Ascension Engine, *not* from the absence of the gods.
4. **Seraphel's Private Goal:** Seeks personal apotheosis into divinity as the "Hidden Tenth," using "Divine Return" rhetoric as camouflage.
5. **Brother Cael / Orinth:** The Laughing Sage alone remained behind on Elonia as caretaker of Lantern Chapel; an observer and witness, never an oracle or puzzle-solver.
6. **Protected Unknowns (`[UNRESOLVED]`):** The Sea Beyond, human origins, ultimate afterlife, whether Myrraen was right, and the blank final chapter of *Ain Elonar* must remain unanswered.
