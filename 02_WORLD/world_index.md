## Canon rules for world content

1. **Present year is c. 1,000 AS.** Draft dates of 1,100 AS are superseded.
2. **Ascendants ≠ Ascensionists.** Modern scholars vs. ancient reformers who built the Engine.
3. **The Soul Crisis** comes from the Engine's wound, not the gods' absence.
4. **Seraphel's goal** is personal apotheosis as the Hidden Tenth.
5. **Brother Cael is Orinth** — observer and witness, never a puzzle-solver.
6. **Protected unknowns** stay unanswered.
