# Relics & Artifacts of Elonia

> Plot-bearing objects. Values are for fencing/insurance, never for buying victory. Full lore stays in `06_ADVENTURE/` chapters.

---

## 1. Lantern of the Last Light
*Wondrous item, artifact (requires attunement by a willing bearer who answered Cael's question honestly)*
- 60 ft bright light. Illusions drop, shapeshifters revealed, the dead speak without screaming.
- Bearer + allies within light: advantage vs charm, frightened, possession.
- Undead within light: disadvantage on attacks; specters/wraiths cannot regenerate.
- **Agency action (1/session):** Present lived evidence in its light — party gains advantage + 30 temp HP (see Ch.12).
- Destroying or selling it is impossible without Cael's grief becoming a continental event. Don't test this.

## 2. Broken Seal of Solcaris + Covenant Seals
*Wondrous item, legendary (pair)*
- Two halves of a solar disk, faint dying heat. Together under Lantern-light: reveal partnership compacts (Solar script) and the Vareth transit cipher.
- Touch: 1d4 radiant to undead, harmless otherwise. Uncalibrated handling: DC 15 Arcana or 2d8 radiant backlash.
- Other Covenant Seals (Grand Lyceum originals): authenticate any Pre-Silence charter as genuine/forged, 1/day each. Cael's bare touch calms any of them instantly `[DM-ONLY]`.

## 3. Keys / Shards of Nythara
*Wondrous item, very rare (set of 3)*
- Star-glass keys cut to Selyra's Clock. One key per Nexus socket; turning all three (action each, 5 rounds held) opens the Star Road shaft.
- As weapons: +1 dagger equivalent, 1d4+1 + 1d6 radiant to constructs/undead. Breaking one seals its socket forever — Convergence +1.

## 4. Memory Leaves
*Wondrous item, uncommon (consumable)*
- Golden aspen leaves holding one mortal's sensory memory (Vale Myrr grove). Press to brow, action: experience 1 minute of their shelter, grief, or joke.
- Effect: remove 1 level of exhaustion or gain inspiration (once per leaf). Clerics/paladins brushing one at need: restore spell slots of 5th or lower (1/day max, Vale Myrr onward at DM discretion).
- Central Leaf (Chair of Silence): Myrraen's parting memory. No boon — only the quote. Never duplicate it.

## 5. Varek's Final Token
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)*
- Bronze door-mark of the Keeper. Bearer + 5 ft aura: resistance to necrotic and cold; advantage on saves vs possession and exhaustion from soul-fields.
- 1/day: *Sanctuary* vs undead/fiends only (DC 15). Token cracks if used to bar a willing soul's passage — Custodians notice.

## 6. Book of Unasked Questions
*Wondrous item, rare (Orinth's gift, at DM discretion — Act III onward)*
- Blank-paged journal in Cael's hand. Writing a genuine question overnight yields no answer — instead advantage on the next Investigation/Insight check pursuing it.
- 1/week: ask the Book about a sealed text; it replies with the right *question* to ask the text's keeper (never the contents).

## 7. Crown of Avarr
*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement; cleanse first)*
- Found tarnished in the Engine Deep (Act VI, at DM discretion). Uncleansed: +2 Int, disadvantage on Wis saves, whispers of certainty.
- Cleansed (Lantern + Vale cairn-rite, 1 hr): +2 Wis, immunity to charm/frightened from celestials/fiends, 1/day *Mind Blank* (self only).
- Wearing it unconsecrated into the Open Veil: Avatar targets you first. It remembers Avarr.

## 8. Catalogue Codex + Index Tablet + Annales of Concord
*Plot documents (25 lb / 3 lb / 8 lb). No magic. Irreplaceable.*
- Codex: hospital charters, irrigation law, star-charts — humanism proof (Act I).
- Tablet: Star Chamber key + mosaic reference (Act I–II).
- Annales: condensed redaction-span chronicle (Act II+). Displaying any under Lantern-light counts as lived evidence (Ch.12).
- If lost: Cartel fence re-sells leaves next morning (fallback rule), never the whole book.

## 9. Spirit-Glass Vials & Condensers
*Wondrous item, varies (evidence first, loot second)*
- Sealed vial: warm, faint faces. Carrying: whispers at night (DC 13 Wis or restless long rest). Opening without Custodian rite: 4d10 psychic + released soul lost `[DM-ONLY: Convergence +1 for Vane if stolen back]`.
- Condenser/Invoices/Stamp (VANE-IL-984): 200–500 gp fence value each as scrap; infinite value as proof. Party should never sell the stamp — Moros buries vials with names.

## 10. Star-Glass Lens
*Wondrous item, common curiosity (25 gp)*
- Cold sliver of fused starlight. As arcane focus: light cantrips reach +10 ft. No key function — despite what Vane buyers claim.
