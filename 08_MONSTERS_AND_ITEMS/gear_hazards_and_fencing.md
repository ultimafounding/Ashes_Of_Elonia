## 2. Faction Hardware `[DM-ONLY]`
- **Soul-Siphon Rifle (Corvin, Ch.8):** +9 to hit, 90 ft: 21 (3d10+5) force + 10 (3d6) necrotic; DC 17 Con or max-HP reduction by necrotic until long rest. Looted rifles crack after 1d4 shots — then evidence only.
- **Siphon-Array (hazard, Bleak Path/Vareth deep):** AC 19, 60 HP per array; destroyed wrong (no valve order): 3d8 psychic backlash 15 ft. Valve order (Vess, DC 16 Thieves'/Arcana x3): quiet, vortex visibly eases, Corvin clock -1 if <5 rounds.
- **Ley-Tether (Engine Deep):** AC 19, 150 HP; struck: *Chain Lightning* DC 18, 10d8. Ritual bleed (DC 19 x3 rounds): quiet, Veil +0. Brute sever: Veil +1.
- **Crucible Field (100 ft):** DC 18 Wis or 4d10 psychic (chorus). Lantern catwalk: safe 10 ft corridor.

## 3. Terrain Hazards by Act
- **Catalogue scree (I):** slide 20 ft + 1d6; pitons negate.
- **Crucible smoke (II):** DC 12 Con or poisoned 1 round; splash 1d10 fire.
- **Fog-Step wind/ice (III):** challenge fail = 2d6 fall; ward chimes on siphon-tech.
- **Soul-wave vent (IV):** 40-ft cold snap, DC 13 Con or 2d6 cold; plaza stampede at Tide 4.
- **Glass-storm (V):** DC 16 Con or 3d10 slashing + blinded 1 hr.
- **Gear-Spire (VI):** DC 18 Acrobatics per platform; temporal static ±1 round; steam 4d10 fire.
- **Veil drag (VI mythic):** 20 ft/round toward tear after Avatar mythic triggers.

## 4. Fencing & Proof Economy
- Stolen text pages: 100–500 gp/page (Cartels pay). Selling *proof* (stamp, invoices, Token, Seal halves): infinite plot value, ~0 gp wisdom — Moros/Custodians/choirs reward with writs, rites, and companies instead.
- Buying forbidden relics the deans would seize: Cartels pay triple; Seraphel pays triple-triple through cutouts `[DM-ONLY: every sale = Convergence +1]`.
