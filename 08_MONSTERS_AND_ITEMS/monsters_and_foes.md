# Act I — Port Meridian & the Shattered Archive

## Giant Rat (CR 1/8)
*Small Beast, unaligned* — **Appears in:** [[session_01_three_days_pay|Session 1]], [[chapter_1_shattered_archive|Chapter 1]]

**AC** 12 • **HP** 7 (2d6) • **Speed** 30 ft.
**STR** 7 (−2) **DEX** 15 (+2) **CON** 11 (+0) **INT** 2 (−4) **WIS** 10 (+0) **CHA** 4 (−3)
**Senses** darkvision 30 ft., passive 10 • **Keen Smell:** advantage on Perception (smell)
**Bite.** +4 to hit, 5 ft., 4 (1d4+2) piercing.
**Tactics.** Swarm the nearest target, flee at half HP. They want the rope, not the party.

## Giant Centipede (CR 1/4)
*Small Beast, unaligned* — **Appears in:** [[session_01_three_days_pay|Session 1]], [[chapter_1_shattered_archive|Chapter 1]]

**AC** 13 • **HP** 4 (1d6+1) • **Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.
**STR** 5 (−3) **DEX** 14 (+2) **CON** 12 (+1) **INT** 1 (−5) **WIS** 7 (−2) **CHA** 3 (−4)
**Senses** blindsight 30 ft., passive 10
**Bite.** +4 to hit, 5 ft., 4 (1d4+2) piercing, and the target must succeed on a **DC 11 Con save** or take **2d6 poison** and be **poisoned until the end of its next turn**.
**Tactics.** Climb the walls, drop on the back rank, flee open flame. They do not focus-fire.

> **Lethality note:** 3d6 poison averages 10.5 and can one-shot a 9 HP PC. Use 2d6 for level 1.

## Skeleton (CR 1/4)

**Vulnerabilities** bludgeoning • **Immunities** poison • **Condition Immunities** exhaustion, poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive 9
**Shortsword.** +4 to hit, 5 ft., 5 (1d6+2) piercing.

## Ghoul, Tunnel-Scourge (CR 1)
*Medium Undead, chaotic evil* — **Appears in:** [[session_02_hall_of_records|Session 2]]

**AC** 12 • **HP** 15 (reduced from 22) • **Speed** 30 ft.
**STR** 13 (+1) **DEX** 15 (+2) **CON** 10 (+0) **INT** 7 (−2) **WIS** 10 (+0) **CHA** 6 (−2)
**Skills** Perception +2 • **Immunities** poison • **Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive 10
**Claws.** +4 to hit, 5 ft., 7 (2d4+2) slashing; **DC 10 Con save** or **paralyzed** for 1 minute (repeat save at end of each turn). **No paralysis on round 1** — it is gorging.
**Bite.** +2 to hit, 5 ft., 9 (2d6+2) piercing.
**Tactics.** Drags, does not execute. Flees at 5 HP toward the far dark.

## Stirge (CR 1/8)
*Tiny Beast, unaligned* — **Appears in:** [[session_03_the_oath|Session 3]], [[chapter_1_shattered_archive|Chapter 1]]

**AC** 14 (natural) • **HP** 2 (1d4) • **Speed** 10 ft., fly 40 ft.
**STR** 4 (−3) **DEX** 16 (+3) **CON** 11 (+0) **INT** 2 (−4) **WIS** 8 (−1) **CHA** 6 (−2)
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive 10
**Blood Drain.** +5 to hit, 5 ft., 5 (1d4+3) piercing, and the stirge attaches. While attached, it does not attack; at the start of each of its turns, the target loses 5 (1d4+3) HP from blood loss. The stirge can detach by spending 5 ft. of movement. A creature can use its action to detach it.
**Tactics.** Drop on the rear rank, attach, drink. Mindless.

## Kael, Cartel Salvage Captain (CR 2)
*Medium Humanoid (Human), neutral evil* — **Appears in:** [[session_05_star_chamber|Session 5]], [[session_06_gilded_docks_inquest|Session 6]]

**AC** 14 (chain shirt) • **HP** 45 (7d8+14) • **Speed** 30 ft.
**STR** 16 (+3) **DEX** 12 (+1) **CON** 14 (+2) **INT** 12 (+1) **WIS** 11 (+0) **CHA** 13 (+1)
**Saves** Str +5, Con +4 • **Skills** Athletics +5, Deception +3, Intimidation +3, Investigation +3
**Senses** passive 10 • **Languages** Common, thieves' cant
**Dirty Fighting (1/turn).** When Kael hits with a melee weapon, the target makes a **DC 12 Con save** or is blinded until the end of its next turn.
**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** Kael makes two attacks: one **Warhammer** and one **Dagger**.
- **Warhammer.** +5 to hit, 5 ft., 7 (1d8+3) bludgeoning; **Versatile** 8 (1d10+3).
- **Dagger.** +5 to hit, 5 ft. or 20/60 ft., 5 (1d4+3) piercing.
- **Topple Pillar (1/encounter).** **DC 15 Athletics** to knock a pillar into the exit. On a success, the collapse begins.
**Tactics.** Offers a deal once; fights defensively; targets the Codex carrier; surrenders at 10 HP if the Thugs are down.

**STR** 12 (+1) **DEX** 14 (+2) **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 8 (−1) **WIS** 12 (+1) **CHA** 8 (−1)
**Resist** cold, necrotic • **Immune** charmed, exhaustion • **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive 11
**Mist-Bound.** Invisible in fog or mist until it attacks; Lantern-light negates.
**Actions**
- **Cold Moan (recharge 5–6).** 15-ft cone, DC 12 Con, 7 (2d6) cold and speed 0 until the end of its next turn.

**STR** 16 (+3) **DEX** 10 (+0) **CON** 16 (+3) **INT** 10 (+0) **WIS** 12 (+1) **CHA** 6 (−2)
