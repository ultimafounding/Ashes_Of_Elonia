## Reef-Crawler (CR 1/2)
*Medium Monstrosity, unaligned* — nest in Meridian reefs, market cages, cliff pools.
**AC:** 14 (barnacle shell) • **HP:** 19 (3d8+6) • **Speed:** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.
**STR:** 14 (+2) DEX 12 (+1) CON 14 (+2) INT 2 (-4) WIS 10 (+0) CHA 4 (-3)
**Skills:** Stealth +3 (in water +5) • **Senses:** blindsight 10 ft., passive 10
**Amphibious. Razor Barnacles:** melee attackers take 2 (1d4) slashing.
**Actions — Multiattack:** 2x Pincer +3, 5 (1d6+2) bludgeoning; grapple on both hits (escape DC 12).
