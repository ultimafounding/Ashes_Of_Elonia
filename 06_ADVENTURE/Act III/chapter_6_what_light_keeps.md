# Chapter 6: What the Light Keeps
> *"The lamp does not judge the dark. It only refuses to leave you in it."*
> **Act III • Levels 10–12 • Sites: Altar of the Lantern, parapets, Vale; night siege**

---
## 1. Lantern Trials (investigation, not attunement shortcut)
Cael permits supervised trials. Lantern: 60 ft bright light; illusions drop; shapeshifters revealed; dead speak without screaming (per `lantern_chapel.md`).
- **Trial 1 — Seal reading:** Under Lantern + DC 15 Religion: Seal halves show paired solar script: partnership compact, not commandment. Copying the script (1 hr) yields the Vareth transit cipher for Act IV.
- **Trial 2 — Crowded stair:** Party member holds Lantern over the Vale at dusk, DC 14 Perception: light catches *crowding* — countless patient shapes queued at a broken stair that is not a door. No identities, no destinations `[UNRESOLVED]`. Cael, quietly: *"The parting did not make that queue. The queue was already there. The parting only closed the kitchens and left the hungry standing."*
- **Trial 3 — Memory shard (courtyard loose stone):** Selyra's voice, weeping, fragmentary: *"...branching... do not bind them to one sky..."* Prophecy-as-possibility; no fixed fate. Links Act V Nythara, do not decode now.
- Each trial +1 Unpassed Tide (resonance draws hunger).

## 2. The Last Council telling (CONFIRMED, not absolved)
If Trust 3 + two vectors, Cael tells it once, by the stove, no grandeur:
- Avarr's pyres were lit by the certain — magistrates praying to Aurelion and Myrraen while burning dissent.
- The Nine divided: Aurelion for continued guidance, Kaelis for freedom though it kill, Myrraen for severance — *"as long as we stand between them and their mistakes, they will never become adults."*
- Orinth stayed: not to rule or rescue, but to witness and keep a lamp. *"If I kept the robes, you'd kneel. Kneeling is the disease."*
- Present both truths: severance caused centuries of agony AND enabled mortal medicine, law, lanthorns, arches. No verdict offered. If party demands one, Cael refuses: *"That page is yours, not mine."*

## 3. Siege of Lantern Chapel (climax, Lv 11–12)
**Unpassed Tide:** spectral horde drawn by resonance (per location file). 6-round parapet defense:
- Rounds 1–2: **2x Wraiths (mist-thinned: HP 45)** + **4x Specters** test the courtyard bell-frame.
- Rounds 3–4: **1x Banshee (voiceless: no wail, HP 50)** + tide surge (DC 14 Con or level of exhaustion from cold grief).
- Rounds 5–6: gate breaks; party + Cael hands on Lantern together (action each, willing). Golden radiance repels horde; no damage to party. Cael fights only with staff + *Sanctuary/Calm* on downed PCs — never blasts the boss.
- **Attunement:** Lantern loaned, not given. Bearer must answer Cael's question honestly: *"What will you do when the light shows you something you wish it hadn't?"* Any honest answer suffices; evasion fails.

## 4. Departure
Dawn: cairns reset, slates stacked. Cael's map-mark to Khar Vareth + letter to **High Custodian Moros** (*"They carry lamplight honestly. — C."*). At crag-foot, Seraphel's courier with Vareth introductions, funds, and one request: *"copy any star-marks or pipe-marks for the villa vault."* Kindness intact; danger invisible.
- **Milestones:** Survive trials + hear telling → **Level 11**. Hold the parapets + receive Lantern → **Level 12**. Open Act IV.
