# Chapter 5: The Hermit of the Mist
> *"Wipe your boots. The mist brings mud, and the floors are a thousand years old."*
> **Act III • Levels 8–10 • Sites: Fog-Step Ascent, Courtyard, Cell, Scribe's Crypt, Vale**

---
## 1. Arrival — Fog-Step Ascent
500 steps, gale + slick ice. Skill challenge (4 successes before 3 fails): DC 14 Athletics (wind), DC 14 Perception (false edge in fog), DC 15 Survival (cairn-marks). Failure: 2d6 fall + **Mist-Ghouls (2x Wraith-spawned: use Specter stats, HP 30, cold moan)** feed on stragglers.
- **Threshold Ward (step 300):** Celestine arch chimes if soul-siphon tech or active necromancy carried. Cael hears it above; makes tea. Note but do not explain.

## 2. Hearth-Right (Trust engine)
Cael's greeting per dossier: boots, stove, bitter mint tea (advantage vs charm/illusion 24 hr from well-water + tea). He asks each PC their greatest unasked question (inspiration for honest answers). Labor earns trust: chop wood, reset slates, copy a logbook page, honor a Vale cairn.
- **Refusals honored:** If party demands the Lantern day one, Cael smiles: *"And what will you light with it, friend? Show me the Seal's story first, and we'll see what the old lamp thinks."* No roll bypasses this — service + evidence only.

## 3. The Three Vectors (deduction, not confession)
- **A — Logbooks (Cell floorboards):** 48 volumes, 42 AS–present, one messy left hand. DC 14 Investigation: weather + recipes + margin grief (*"M. wept at the table again. I said nothing. What could I say?"*). Never signed Orinth. Let players count.
- **B — Seal-touch:** Uncalibrated Broken Seal flares near the Altar (DC 15 Arcana to steady; failure: 2d8 radiant backlash). If Cael takes it bare-handed to help, it calms instantly; whisper in Celestine (Comprehend/DC 16 Religion): *"Greeting, Sage. The First Flame remembers you."* Cael covers: *"Old brass likes warm hands."*
- **C — The Slip:** DC 16 Insight/History during tea-story: Cael calls the Solcaris architect by a childhood nickname found only in the Annales' footnote. If pressed, he laughs too long, pours more tea.
- **Cael rule:** He answers questions with questions. *"Was Myrraen wrong?" → "If your mother never let you touch the stove, would you learn fire?"* Never confirms until confronted with two vectors.

## 4. Vale + Crypt (Silence groundwork)
- **Vale of Remembrance:** Thousands of cairns for unrecorded dead. Tending one (+1 Trust). A **restless specter (Fall of Harad Veyr)** begs its name (DC 14 History or Lantern-light): *"We prayed while the pyres lit. Tell me — did they hear?"* No answer given; record the question.
- **Scribe's Crypt:** 72 sarcophagi; Memorial Wall (*carry light so others see where they step*). Final dictation slate (DC 13): *"The stair broke before the parting. The doors, not the door-keeper, failed. M. saw the crack and feared the cage more than the dark."* File as investigated, not confirmed cosmology.

## 5. Milestone
Two vectors deduced + Trust 2 → **Level 9**. Confrontation scene played honestly (Cael's unmasking line per dossier; no kneeling) → **Level 10**. Lantern trials open in Chapter 6.
