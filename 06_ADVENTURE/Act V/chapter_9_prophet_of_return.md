# Chapter 9: The Prophet of Return
> *"You carry pipes and ledgers to a festival. They carry candles. Be kind. Be exact. Be unmoved."*
> **Act V • Levels 15–16 • Sites: Solcaris Resonant Pylons, Whispering Wastes, Dreamkeeper waystation**

---
## 1. Solcaris Under Occupation
White-and-gold processions hold the pylon field. **Returner Vanguard Zealots (CR 6)** + pilgrim thousands. Inner Circle crews (Void-Artificers CR 7) tune pylons behind cordons. Direct assault = massacre + Convergence +1 (martyrs). Play the crowd as grieving kin, not villains.

## 2. Objectives (heist + persuasion, not siege)
- **A — Pylon key:** Eastern Tether pylon (Solcaris). DC 18 Arcana/Thieves' to extract tuning key during hymn-crescendo (cover noise). Artificer patrol: 1x Void-Artificer + 2x Zealots per shift; rotation 20 min (Dreamkeeper signal-lamp).
- **B — Star Road cipher:** Survey tent holds transit-cipher + Corvin's continental orders (*"master alone ascends; hold the faithful at the pylons"*). Stealth DC 17 or parley DC 18 Persuasion with defecting stoker ** Pell** (brother siphoned; will trade cipher for spirit-glass burial per Vareth rite).
- **C — The city (optional, hard):** Public disputation at the dial. DC 20 Persuasion with stamped pipe + invoices displayed via Lantern-light (dead speak calmly, corroborate). Success: council withholds municipal key (Convergence -1). Failure: stones thrown, Zealots emboldened (Convergence +1). Never punish the crowd — punish only the lie. Cael's letter read here grants advantage: *"They loved you enough to leave. Love them enough to tell truth gently."*

## 3. Wastes Transit (pressure valve + omen)
Whispering Wastes: glass-storm (DC 16 Con or 3d10 slashing + blinded 1 hr), void-automaton scout (1x **Stone Golem-lite: HP 120, star-glass fists**). Convergence pulse mid-transit: sky violet 6 seconds, Mirror-Pool style blood-ripple in canteens. Dreamkeeper waystation: blindfolded monk gives pylon-geometry truth (*"they point down"*) + cold-weather seals for Nythara.

## 4. Milestone
Pylon key + cipher secured → **Level 16**. Council turned or bypassed without massacre → Front +1.
