# Chapter 1: The Shattered Archive
> *"Three days' pay to carry rope and count stones. What could go wrong eighty feet under a warehouse?"*
> **Act I • Levels 1–2 • Hub: Port Meridian • Sites: Warehouse Nine, Catalogue Descent, Hall of Records, Witness Scriptorium**

---
## 1. Setup & Player-Facing Hook `[PLAYER-FACING]`
- **Patron:** **Helena Voss**, salvage factor, hires party at the **Salt-Lantern Tavern** for a 3-day private charter: clear vermin, shore timber, map the fault beneath **Warehouse Nine**.
- **Pay:** 15 gp each + 10% of certified scrap. Charter writ covers only *salvage*, not *antiquities* — Admiralty fine if caught fencing relics.
- **Companion:** **Arlen Marr** (use stat block in `04_NPCS/major_allies_and_rivals.md`) accompanies as recorder. He translates High Elonian at half speed unless protected while working (10 min per inscription).
- **Public belief:** Pre-Silence cellars hold scrap copper and nesting vermin. Ascensionists were death-cultists — do not touch altar-stones `[PLAYER-FACING]`.

## 2. DM Truth `[DM-ONLY]`
Chambers 1.1–1.3 are an Ascensionist civic archive (c. 1,100 BS), not a cult crypt. Cartel **Breakers** under **Vance Rorik** surveyed Chamber 1.3 within the last month (lamp-oil rag, boot-heels, snapped lockpick) and will return in Chapter 2. **Seraphel** has no direct hand here yet — he watches via his townhouse agent after the party surfaces.

## 3. Keyed Dungeon
### 1.1 The Catalogue Descent (steep rubble incline, dim)
**Read-aloud:** *Rope creaks above. Broken steps fall eighty feet into dark that smells of wet lime and old paper.*
- **Hazard:** Loose scree. DC 12 Dex save or slide 20 ft, 1d6 bludgeoning. Pitons + DC 10 Athletics negates.
- **Encounter (Lv 1):** 2x **Giant Centipedes** + 1x **Giant Rat swarm-flavor** nesting in timber shoring. Timbers creak — DC 13 Perception warns.
- **Clue A (Environmental):** Bas-relief of robed figures with sickles, scales, compasses tending fields/hospitals — no skulls. DC 11 History/Religion: not cult iconography.

### 1.2 Hall of Records (lectern rows, dust 2 ft deep)
**Read-aloud:** *Stone desks in dead-straight rows. Dust lies like snow. Something has walked here — a furrow cuts the dust to the far door.*
- **Exploration:** Dust preserves tracks. DC 13 Survival/Investigation: **fresh iron boot-heel marks over ancient dust** + broken modern steel lockpick + grease-rag smelling of New Ilya lamp oil `[DM-ONLY: modern surveyors]`.
- **Encounter (Lv 1–2):** 1x **Ghoul (tunnel-scourge, weakened: HP 15, no paralysis on first round)** + 2x **Skeletons (reassembled archive servitors, non-magical)**. Ghoul gorges on old Breaker left behind — wears Cartel split-coin token (fallback Vector C).
- **Clue B (Documentary):** Petrified ledger leaf: hospital grain budgets, inoculation rota. Arlen translates in 10 min: *"Ward Three — fever beds: 40. Tithe: none. Oath-bound."*

### 1.3 Witness Scriptorium (sealed brass pneumatic door, jammed)
**Read-aloud:** *A round brass door, green with age, hisses half-open. Inside: a dry reading room. Ink-pots still stoppered. A vow cut deep above the desk.*
- **Door:** DC 14 Strength (crowbar advantage) or DC 13 Thieves' Tools (Cartel pick snapped inside gives advantage to notice). Failure by 5+: hiss alerts 1.2 survivors/restless stirge.
- **The Oath of the Scriptorium `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`:** High Elonian inscription. Arlen needs 30 min + DC 13 History aid:
  > *"Not to usurp the heavens, but to ensure mortal children walk without weeping when the hearth grows cold. We keep account, we mend bone, we measure star — that no prayer need be spent for bread."*
  Effect: **CONFIRMS Mystery 1 Stage 3** — Ascensionists were humanist reformers.
- **Clue C (Social):** If party brings leaf/rubbing to Arlen, he breaks: *"The Exchange masters told me to log this as cult debris and sell the vellum by weight. I wouldn't."* Points to Brass Astrolabe backup copy (fallback).
- **Treasure (certified salvage):** 120 gp scrap copper, *Potion of Healing* x2 in Healer's cache, **Catalogue Index Tablet** (plot key to Star Chamber), cryptic schematic: concentric rings + conduit lines — **Engine foreshadow, do not explain** `[DM-ONLY]`.
- **Soul Crisis rumor `[PLAYER-FACING]`:** Dying Breaker (if stabilized) mutters of *"glass-faced ghosts down by the Reefs."* No mechanics yet.
