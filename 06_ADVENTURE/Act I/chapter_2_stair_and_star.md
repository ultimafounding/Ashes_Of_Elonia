# Chapter 2: The Stair and the Star
> *"The Archive goes down. The road goes up. Both end under the same stars."*
> **Act I • Levels 2–4 • Sites: Sea Cliff Road, Star Chamber, Gilded Docks Inquest**

---
## 1. Setup `[PLAYER-FACING]`
Party has the **Catalogue Index Tablet** but the Scriptorium floor collapsed behind them (Cartel powder or shifting fault — leave ambiguous). The Tablet points to the lower vault: accessible only via the old cliff stairstep from the **Sea Cliff Road**, then back under the bay to the **Star Chamber**.

Helena secures a 2-day overland charter. Arlen decodes the Tablet overnight: *"When Selyra's Eye opens, the door remembers."*

## 2. DM Truth `[DM-ONLY]`
- The **Star Chamber** holds the **Catalogue Codex** (hospital charters, irrigation law, star-charts) + a transit-map floor mosaic hinting at linked Engine conduits continent-wide. **Do not name the Engine** — describe only *"copper-veined lines joining Port Meridian to inland nodes."*
- Cartel strike-team (Breakers) races the party via the original Warehouse shaft.
- **Seraphel** intervenes only at the Inquest, as benevolent savior (Law 5: make him indispensable).

## 3. Part A — Sea Cliff Road (overland, 6 miles)
**Read-aloud:** *Salt wind. Basalt steps older than the Admiralty, cut for feet that walked sure in the dark.*
- **Skill challenge (3 successes before 2 fails):** Gale gusts DC 12 Athletics/Acrobatics; crumbled step DC 13 Perception to spot; reef-crawler nest DC 12 Stealth or face 2x **Reef-Crawlers (use Giant Crab stats, ambush)**.
- **Social vector:** Wrecked Cartel mule: discarded New Ilya oilskin + survey chit signed with split-coin mark. Prisoner **Cartel scout (Commoner + shortbow)** if interrogated (DC 13 Intimidation/Persuasion): *"Rorik pays triple for bound books, no questions. A gold man in Ilya coin pays him triple-triple for star-glass and pipe-metal."* `[DM-ONLY: Seraphel buys via cutout — never confirm]`.
- **Soul Crisis rumor:** Low-tide pool shows drowned sailor's shade flickering violet, shadow detached. DC 12 Arcana: *"passage stutters."* No cure at this tier.

## 4. Part B — The Star Chamber (climactic vault)
**Read-aloud:** *A dome of lapis and silver wire. Constellations burn cold overhead. On a stone plinth, a book bound in salt-swollen oak — dustless, as if the room held its breath for a thousand years.*
- **Puzzle — Selyra's Eye:** Dome has 3 rotatable silver rings (DC 12 Investigation to grasp). Tablet shows alignment: Eye-star + Helm + Plough. Each wrong alignment: 1d8 radiant backlash + grinding warns Cartels. Correct: plinth unlocks (audible *click*, dust falls).
- **Documentary payoff `[ESTABLISHED CANON]`:** **Catalogue Codex**: intake ledgers, quarantine law, midwife rota, star-measured aqueduct gradients. Margin note: *"If the gods close the granary, we will build our own."* Second confirmation of humanist thesis.
- **Environmental payoff:** Floor mosaic transit-map: Meridian node joined by copper lines to 5 dark nodes inland. Helena (DC 11 Navigator's Tools): *"That's not a trade map. That's a machine plan."* Leave it there.
- **Climax combat (Lv 3–4, Hard):** **Cartel strike-team** breaches from shaft as Codex lifts: 1x **Cartel Enforcer (CR 4, see `05_FACTIONS/the_archive_cartels.md`)**, 2x **Thugs**, 1x **Scout (crossbow, on ledge)**. Enforcer offers once: *"Book for 400 gold and walk."* If refused, topples pillar (DC 13 Dex or 2d6 bludgeoning, chamber begins collapse — 5 rounds to exit with Codex).
- **Treasure:** Codex (plot artifact, 25 lb — needs protection), *Star-glass Lens* (25 gp curiosity, cold to the touch), 200 gp pre-Silence coinage, *Scroll of Lesser Restoration*.

## 5. Part C — Gilded Docks Inquest (social finale)
Admiralty magistrates impound the Codex under salvage-vs-antiquities law. Magistrate **Ilya Renn** charges unlicensed antiquarianism (fine 500 gp / impressment).
- **Skill challenge:** Legal defense DC 14 Persuasion (Helena grants advantage with charter log), bribe 200 gp (risk +1 Censor's Shroud later), Arlen testimony DC 12.
- **Seraphel's entrance `[DM-ONLY: staged benevolence]`:** Regardless of rolls, before verdict, **Lord Seraphel** (see `04_NPCS/lord_seraphel.md`) arrives by carriage, pays clearance tax in full, cites Concord of Meridian salvage precedent. Awards party **full fellowship to the Grand Lyceum, New Ilya** + letters + 100 gp stipend each.
- **Roleplay key:** Warm, peer-like, asks piercing question about the mosaic lines — logs answer privately. Never takes Codex custody by force; asks to *"sponsor authentication."* Let party keep it.

## 6. Milestones & Hand-off
- Survive Sea Cliff Road → **Level 3**. Secure Codex + survive Inquest → **Level 4**. Close Act I clocks; open Act II hook: travel with Seraphel's retinue to New Ilya.
- **World-state shift:** per `act_I_overview.md` §5 — party holds primary humanism evidence; Cartels thwarted; Admiralty paid; Seraphel trusted patron.
