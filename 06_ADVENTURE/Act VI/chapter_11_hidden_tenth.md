# Chapter 11: The Hidden Tenth
> *"I saw what freedom gave you. I will not apologize for wanting to end it."*
> **Act VI • Levels 18–19 • Sites: Gear-Spire, Tethers, Crucible, Dais**

---
## 1. Descent — Gear-Spire of the First
3-mile vertical clockwork: inverted gravity zones, temporal static (spell resolves ±1 round), steam-vents (DC 18 Dex, 4d10 fire). **Ascension Automatons (2x Iron Golem-lite: HP 150, Vane codes demanded)** — codes from Ch.9 cipher grant bypass; else combat while falling (platform DC 18 Acrobatics or 3d10 fall onto cogs).

## 2. Four Ley-Tethers (weaken the god-to-be)
N / S / E / W spirit-glass conduits (AC 19, 150 HP each, *Chain Lightning* DC 18 10d8 on struck). Options per tether: ritual bleed (DC 19 Arcana/Religion, 3 rounds, quiet, Veil +0) or brute sever (damage, Veil +1). Each severed: Crucible dims; Seraphel loses 50 HP + 1 siphon-heal; party gains Crucible-shield.
- Tether guardians: N: Void-Artificer pair; S: Corvin's last stokers + bone-naga; E: Zealot wall (pleading, non-lethal preferred — Front die to convert); W: automated star-glass warden.

## 3. Soul Crucible (500,000 held)
Adamantine sphere, howling stasis. Within 100 ft unshielded: DC 18 Wis or 4d10 psychic (chorus). Lantern-bearer can walk the catwalk safely (light parts grief 10 ft). Freeing now = Veil +2 early (do not). Note: faces calm near Lantern — evidence for Ch.12 argument.

## 4. Apotheosis Dais — Seraphel, the Ascending Tenth
Obsidian steps, cataract of radiance, twelve platinum rings. Half-ascended Seraphel: use **Archmage + legendary (3/round: siphon-heal 40 HP consuming tether charge, radiant lance +14 4d10+7, *Counterspell*), HP 350 base** (-50 per severed tether, +100 if Convergence 8). Corvin (if alive) holds rings (AC 18, 90 HP) — killing him first drops Seraphel's concentration shield.
- **Bargain (once, sincere):** *"Join me as archons. No child starves. No soul lost. No dark. I will bear the certainty so you need not."* No trick in the offer — price is everything. Let it land.
- **Duel beats:** rings shatter at 2/3, 1/3 HP; Crucible cracks; tethers whip free. Dying: no curse. Whisper: *"I am sorry you insist on suffering. I truly... wanted the weight to end."* Soul releases north-wind, mortal, unbound `[UNRESOLVED destination]`.
- **Milestone:** Fell the Tenth → **Level 19**, long rest impossible (Veil screams). Step through.
