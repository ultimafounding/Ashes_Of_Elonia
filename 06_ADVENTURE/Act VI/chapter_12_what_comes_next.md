# Chapter 12: Of What Comes Next
> *"Then walk, children. Walk into the dark — and build your own dawn."*
> **Act VI • Level 20 • Sites: Open Veil, Blank Altar**

---
## 1. Manifestation (no initiative yet)
Siphons snap; 500,000 stream past like low sun through ice. Pressure tears the Veil: pearlescent fissure, lilies + rain smell. The **Avatar of the Last Mercy** coalesces (see `04_NPCS/the_avatar_of_the_last_mercy.md` — run maternal, never cruel). It speaks first, gently:
- *"Why do you cling to knives? Ten centuries of dust, and freedom bought you graveyards."*
- *"I come to take the weight. Close your eyes. No more winter."*
- *"You fight for the right to bleed. Why do you love your wounds?"*
Let each PC answer or stay silent. Record answers — they are ammunition.

## 2. Mythic Combat (Lv 20)
Run per dossier: AC 22, HP 450 + mythic 400, resists/immunities, DC 22 saves, Stasis aura, Shroud petrify, mythic actions after 0 HP. Gravity drags 20 ft/round toward Veil post-mythic. Lair laws active.
- **Agency mechanic (not damage bypass):** Once per PC, presenting lived evidence as an action (Lantern, Codex leaf, Moros writ, Memory Leaf, Dreamkeeper branching, Vale cairn-stone) grants party-wide advantage + 30 temp HP + Avatar -1 legendary resist (min 1). Cap 4. Their Act I–V choices fight here.
- **Final round:** At mythic 0 HP, Avatar kneels, core exposed. One PC must declare agency aloud (action): hold *Ain Elonar* or Lantern, accept suffering + responsibility. Any honest wording works; evasion fails. Avatar sighs, touches brow, dissolves: *"Then walk..."*
