## 1. Executive Summary & Dramatic Identity

- **Level Band:** 18 to 20 (Tier 4: Masters of the World).
- **Geographic Setting:** Star Road shaft → Gear-Spire → Four Ley-Tethers → Soul Crucible → Apotheosis Dais → Open Veil + Blank Altar.
- **Narrative Phase:** Cosmic Climax, Philosophical Synthesis, Unwritten Future.
- **Player-Facing Trajectory:** None left to manage — sirens, quakes, fractured sky. Only descent.
- **The Core Question:** *Who has the right to author the future?*
- **The Required Turn `[DM-ONLY]`:**
  1. *Seraphel falls as mortal:* intellect + sincerity do not license apotheosis. He dies grieving, not cackling.
  2. *Certainty manifests:* severed siphons + released pressure tear the Veil; the Avatar coalesces — protection without freedom.
  3. *No imposed answer replaces it:* victory = declaration of agency + blank page left blank until players write it.
- **Lair laws (per `the_engine_deep_and_the_veil.md`):** unspoken-words surge (6th+ spells: nat 1 = 6d6 radiant 30 ft); resonant mortality (heals max, whispers 1 round); unraveling law (teleport/time 25% fail).
