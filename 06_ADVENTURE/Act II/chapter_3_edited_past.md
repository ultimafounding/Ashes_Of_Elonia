# Chapter 3: The Edited Past
> *"Every shelf agrees. That is the first lie."*
> **Act II • Levels 4–6 • Sites: Scriptorium of the Blue Rose, Sunken Conclave, Glass Kettle**

---
## 1. Setup `[PLAYER-FACING]`
Fellowship audience at the **Grand Lyceum**: registrars praise the Catalogue Codex, then route authentication to committee — 6 weeks' delay. **Arlen Marr** notices the finding aid: everything 1,200 BS–0 AS reads *"In Repair / Lost in Fire / Chancellor's Privilege"* across all eight faculties.

**Lysa Corren** slips a cracked-hourglass chalk-mark onto a PC's door: midnight, **Sunken Conclave**, come alone-ish.

## 2. DM Truth `[DM-ONLY]`
The gap is active policy from the **Council of Deans** via the **Athenaeum Null**. **Dean Vale** believes quarantine prevents panic (see `new_ilya.md` §3 defense — play sincere, not sneering). **Seraphel** funds both sides: endows the Lyceum, buys Lysa's smuggled rubbings at triple rates through cutouts. His letters to the party always close asking for *"resonance frequencies, conduit alloys, key geometries — that the technicians may preserve them properly."*

## 3. Part A — Blue Rose Stacks (investigation)
- **Documentary vector:** DC 14 Investigation across two faculties: identical red *"IN REPAIR"* stamps, same hand, same week, different centuries of material. DC 15 History: fire-loss cannot synchronize across marble towers.
- **Environmental vector:** Basement bindery: razor-scored vellum edges under loupe (DC 13 Perception), silver-crucible slag in waste bins smelling of burnt hide-glue.
- **Social vector:** Bonded scribe **Tommick** (Commoner): DC 13 Persuasion (buy his debt-slip, 30 gp) or Lysa's introduction → whispers shift-times of the *"black bindery that never sleeps."*
- **Obstacles:** Censor patrol (4x **Silver-Masked Inquisitors — use Veteran stats minus Multiattack, CR 2 each, rod of Detect Magic**); failure = DC 14 Deception or +1 Censor's Shroud. Keepers of Dawn picket preaches *"hubris summons Silence again"* — social complication, not combat.
- **Soul Crisis foreshadow (once):** Census folio left on a desk: cremation-release failure 14% upriver. Lysa: *"Ask the Deans why the dead linger. Watch them change subject."* No mechanics, no confirmation.

## 4. Part B — Sunken Conclave (alliance)
Midnight symposium on damp benches. Radical students debate Silence-as-mercy vs cowardice `[DISPUTED — do not resolve]`.
- **Trust challenge:** Lysa tests party: return a seized pamphlet bundle (Stealth DC 13 past porter) or defend a student from porters (nonlethal social/combat). Each success +1 Lysa's Trust.
- **Payoff at Trust 2+:** Lysa shows smuggled rubbing — conduit strut diagram with alloy callouts, torn from a Null-bound original. *"The thing under the earth had pipes. Your mosaic wasn't a map. It was a machine."* (Engine LEARNED, Stage 1.)
- **Payoff at Trust 3:** Pump-station key + Inquisitor rotation + bonded-scribe contact for Ch.4. Glass Kettle becomes safehouse (forged passes, 10 gp each).
- **Seraphel scene (mandated):** Carriage ride after Conclave. Warm, funny, incisive. Warns of spies (true), offers 200 gp research grant (real), asks to *"keep the technical leaves with my vault — safer than a tavern mattress."* If refused, smiles, drops it. `[DM-ONLY: +1 Convergence if handed over; never punish refusal openly.]`

## 5. Milestone
Prove policy-gap + secure Conclave alliance → **Level 5** (mid-chapter, after Trust 2). Secure Null key + grant → **Level 6**. Ch.4 hook: descend via Under-Canal pump station.
