# Chapter 4: The Null Vaults
> *"Below the library that remembers everything is the library that remembers truly."*
> **Act II • Levels 6–8 • Site: The Athenaeum Null (pump plunge → Antechamber → Ashes → Nullity → Solcaris)**

---
## 1. Approach — Under-Canal Pump Station
Sewer-pump disguised hydraulic lift (Lysa's key + DC 14 Thieves' Tools or bonded-scribe window). Failure: noise → +1 Censor's Shroud, guards alerted. Descent 200 ft, water-pressure hiss.

## 2. Keyed Vaults
### 4.1 Iron Antechamber (checkpoint)
**Read-aloud:** *Black iron. Four statues with winter in their eyes. Silver masks turn as one.*
- **Guard:** 2x **Clockwork Censors (use Shield Guardian stats, HP 60, silencing aura 10 ft: verbal spells DC 13 Con or fail)** + 2x **Inquisitors**. Rotation sheet (Lysa) gives 10-min window; bonded scribe can extend with false alarm (one use).
- **Options:** Stealth DC 15 past, forged writs DC 15 Deception, or loud breach (alarm → Antechamber reinforces 4.2).

### 4.2 Scriptorium of Ashes (burn-pits)
Rows of desks; scribes razor pages into silver crucibles. Do not harm scribes if avoidable — they are bonded debt-labor, cower.
- **Vectors:** Redaction desks prove policy (Vector A); slag + razor trays prove method (Vector B); scribe foreman **Osta** surrenders shift-log if promised freedom (Vector C, DC 14 Persuasion).
- **Hazard:** Crucible splash (1d10 fire) if combat near desks; smoke DC 12 Con or poisoned 1 round.

### 4.3 Vault of Nullity (sealed originals, lead-glass)
- **The Ascension Papers `[DM-ONLY: Engine LEARNED]`:** Single bound folio, one passage to read aloud once:
  > *"The Engine breathes beneath the provinces. Conduit the Ninth failed at Solcaris; the lower ways tore like wet linen. We sealed what we could. Let no hand restart it."*
  Close the book. No further explanation. Allow Arlen a stunned silence.
- **The Inquest of Avarr (foreshadow Act III):** Execution orders signed by praying magistrates invoking Aurelion and Myrraen. *"They burned while praying. The gods said nothing."* File under debate, not conclusion.
- **Encounter (drain resources, not boss):** 1x **Null Archivist (Mage, abjuration, CR 6)** + 1x **Censor** arrive on timer (6 rounds after entry). Archivist fights to destroy cases, not to kill — targets glass with *Shatter*.

### 4.4 Vault of Solcaris (Broken Seal)
Heavy iron sanctum, DC 16 Thieves' Tools or Archivist's key. Inside: **Broken Seal of Solcaris** — solar disk snapped in two, faint dying heat (1d4 radiant to undead on touch, harmless otherwise). Take both halves + *Annales of Concord* (condensed chronicle proving redaction span).

## 3. Escape — Flooded Catacombs (climax)
Alarm sounds; **Dean Vale** arrives with 4x Inquisitors at 4.2 as party exits. Vale does not gloat — delivers defense speech (see location file), offers amnesty for the books: *"Give me the Seal and walk as fellows. Publish the Codex. Bury the Papers."* Genuine offer.
- **Chase:** Collapsing sluice + rising water: 4-round skill challenge — DC 14 Athletics (current), DC 13 Survival (route), DC 14 Strength (flood-gate). Lysa's cell holds a skiff at the outflow if Trust 3.
- **If captured:** Vale seizes copies, party keeps originals via Lysa swap — never total loss (fallback rule).

## 4. Aftermath & Exile
Warrants posted by dawn; Lyceum fellowship revoked. Seraphel opens his estate: hot food, physicians, lawyers, masterwork field gear + crag maps (per register). Studies the Seal *reverently, briefly*, returns it. Presses once on schematic custody, accepts refusal gracefully.
- **Hand-off:** *"The Seal is dead metal without the Lantern that lit it. My maps mark Lantern Chapel, Weeping Ridge. Take my carriage as far as the crags."* Supplies + letters + Arlen begs to come (take him if party wants).
- **Milestones:** Seize Annales + Seal → **Level 7**. Escape + accept exile → **Level 8**. Open Act III.
