## 1. Arrival — Toll of Remembrance `[PLAYER-FACING]`
Court of Registry demands not gold but names of parents/grandparents on slips. Refusal = denied entry; false names DC 15 Insight by scribes (offense). Cael's letter to Moros bypasses queue — midnight consultation same night.

## 2. Moros Briefing (statistical horror)
Moros (see `04_NPCS/major_allies_and_rivals.md` + `khar_vareth.md`): exhausted, precise. Shows registry delta: **38% of rites return unpassed within 30 days**; two-year projection = total collapse. *"I do not ask you to comfort us. I ask you to descend and look."*
- Grants: descent rite (spirit projection), Seal-cipher copy access, embalmer guide **Sefra**.
- Warns: Vane "consultants" maintain lower pumps. *"Their machines hum when the dead scream. I have stopped asking why in public."*

## 3. Surface Vectors
- **Plaza:** Twilight names weep cold dew, violet phosphorescence. DC 14 Arcana: stutter, not haunting. Panic risk: preaching here +1 Soul Tide.
- **Terraces:** Vat crisis — corpse whispers Old Concord syllables; fingernail soulway frost. DC 15 Medicine/Religion: passage interrupted mid-transit, not soul refusal. Sefra: *"They whisper of stairs and pipes. I charted which vats scream loudest — nearest the deep shafts."* (map to descent shaft).
- **Necropolis surface:** Twitching sarcophagus — half-siphoned spirit screams *"golden thief, white hair"* if Lantern-calmed (DC 14). Custodians beg silence to avoid panic — tension: truth vs stability (Freedom/Protection beat).
- **Vane smugglers (night):** 4x mercenaries load lead-lined *"Medical Glass"* crates onto desert skiff. Intercept (CR 11 skirmish) yields **pressurized spirit-glass canister (warm, faint faces)** + invoice to northern refinery. Letting it leave: Corvin +1, Convergence +1.

## 4. Seraphel Interlude (donor mask, last wearing)
Seraphel tours the Terraces with magistrates, funds a new reliquary, praises the party publicly: *"My fellows from Meridian — the only honest scholars left."* Privately presses once for Lantern readings + Seal cipher: *"that my engineers may tune the stabilizers."* Handing full cipher over: Corvin +1. Partial/redacted: no penalty, Seraphel smiles tightly.
- **Brass scarab spy:** DC 17 Perception; emits Corvin's frequency if captured (tracks to deep shaft).

## 5. Descent Rite
Custodian projection ritual (1 hr, incense + fasting; bodies guarded by Sefra + 2 Custodians). Projected stats = normal + resistance to cold/necrotic; silver cord HP 10 (severed = snap back, 1 exhaustion). Lantern projects with bearer.
- **Milestone:** Registry proof + vat map + descent → **Level 13**.
