# Milestone Progression: Ashes of Elonia

> *"Progression in Elonia is measured by truth uncovered, burdens carried, and the irreversible weight of choices made."*  
> **Dungeon Master Architecture • Levels 1 through 20**

---

## 1. Milestone Progression Philosophy

In *Ashes of Elonia*, character advancement is governed strictly by narrative milestones rather than experience points (XP). 

### Operational Rules for Advancement
1. **Investigation Over Extermination:** A level is earned by solving a historical contradiction, unmasking an institutional cover-up, or securing a mythic threshold—never by clearing arbitrary monster counts.
2. **Pacing Synchronized with the Six Acts:** Levels align cleanly with the narrative spine, ensuring party capabilities (spell slots, teleportation, resurrection) do not trivialize investigations before the setting is prepared for them.
3. **World-State Feedback:** Every level advancement corresponds with a noticeable shift in how the world perceives the party, their faction standing, and their access to restricted archives and magical relics.

## 3. Level-by-Level Milestone Progression Register

### Tier 1: Local Adventurers (Act I — Port Meridian)

#### Level 1 → Level 2: Into the Shattered Vaults
- **Narrative Milestone:** Breaching the collapsed foundation of Warehouse Nine, surviving the Catalogue Descent's scree and vermin, and reading the boot-worn dust of the Hall of Records for what it is: a modern trail.
- **World State Shift:** The party earns the professional trust of Helena Voss and junior archivist Arlen Marr. They are officially registered as licensed salvage excavators.
- **Key Resources:** Access to basic salvage equipment; first fragments of uncatalogued Pre-Silence script.

#### Level 2 → Level 3: The Witness Scriptorium
- **Narrative Milestone:** Forcing the brass door and translating the *Oath of the Scriptorium*, proving the ancient Ascensionists were legitimate civic reformers — and pocketing the Catalogue Index Tablet.
- **World State Shift:** The party discovers fresh modern bootprints, snapped lockpicks, and New Ilya lamp-oil in the dust, realizing they are racing an unknown modern competitor.
- **Key Resources:** 2nd-level spells unlocked; the Index Tablet points at the Star Chamber.

#### Level 3 → Level 4: The Star Chamber & Seraphel's Patronage
- **Narrative Milestone:** Walking the Sea Cliff Road, aligning Selyra's Eye, lifting the intact *Catalogue Codex*, defeating the Archive Cartel strike force, and standing trial at the Gilded Docks Inquest — where Lord Seraphel pays their tax and sponsors them.
- **World State Shift:** Act I concludes. Lord Seraphel sponsors the party, providing travel warrants, lavish stipends, and letters of introduction to the Grand Lyceum of New Ilya.
- **Key Resources:** Feat / Ability Score Improvement; party gains an elite carriage and formal patron benefits from House Vane.

---

### Tier 2: Heroes of the Realm (Act II & Act III)

#### Level 4 → Level 5: The Grand Lyceum of New Ilya (Start of Act II)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Presenting the *Catalogue Codex* at the university symposium, proving the synchronized 1,200 BS–0 AS gap is policy rather than fire-loss, and earning the Sunken Conclave alliance.
- **World State Shift:** Tier 2 transition. Characters gain regional notoriety as daring intellectual adventurers. The university deans eye them with suspicion.
- **Key Resources:** 3rd-level spells (*Fireball*, *Revivify*, *Dispel Magic*); Extra Attack for martials. Note: *Revivify* begins exhibiting strange cosmetic soul-flickers.

#### Level 5 → Level 6: The Key to the Null
- **Narrative Milestone:** Winning Lysa Corren's trust, securing the Under-Canal pump-station key and patrol rotations, and taking Seraphel's research grant and warning — while he asks, gently, for custody of all technical schematics.
- **World State Shift:** The party holds a door into the black bindery and a patron who pays triple for keys. Both facts itch.
- **Key Resources:** Forged passes and the Glass Kettle safehouse; first sight of a smuggled conduit-strut rubbing — the thing under the earth had pipes.

#### Level 6 → Level 7: The Infiltration of Athenaeum Null
- **Narrative Milestone:** Passing the Iron Antechamber, reading the redaction desks and crucible slag of the Scriptorium of Ashes, and opening the lead-glass cases: the Ascension Papers (the Engine existed and failed) and the Inquest of Avarr.
- **World State Shift:** The party identifies the existence of the *Ascension Engine*. Its full consequence stays buried — one sealed passage, read once.
- **Key Resources:** Recovery of the *Broken Seal of Solcaris* and the *Annales of Concord*; university censors initiate active surveillance and legal warrants against the party.

#### Level 7 → Level 8: Escape from the Ivory Spire
- **Narrative Milestone:** Refusing or enduring Dean Vale's amnesty offer, escaping the ambush of the Athenaeum Inquisitors, surviving the flooded catacombs beneath New Ilya, and securing safe harbor at Seraphel's country villa.
- **World State Shift:** Act II concludes. The party leaves civilized safety behind, designated outlaws by the Republic of Ilya, heading into the rugged crags of the Weeping Ridge.
- **Key Resources:** 4th-level spells unlocked; Seraphel gifts the party masterwork survival gear and navigational maps to Lantern Chapel.

#### Level 8 → Level 9: The Threshold of Lantern Chapel (Start of Act III)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Climbing the Fog-Step Ascent under the chiming ward, earning hearth-right through labor and honest questions, and assembling two of the three vectors: the 42 AS–present logbooks in one hand, the Seal calming at a bare touch, the architect called by a childhood nickname.
- **World State Shift:** The campaign shifts from academic intrigue to mythic discovery. The party rests in a place untouched by the panic of the modern world.
- **Key Resources:** Cael's hearth: bitter mint tea and ice-cold well-water (advantage on saves against charm and illusion for 24 hours); character downtime.

#### Level 9 → Level 10: The Sage Unmasked
- **Narrative Milestone:** Confronting Brother Cael with the evidence and hearing him set down the kettle: he is Orinth, the Laughing Sage — followed by the Vale cairns, the Scribe's final dictation, and the first supervised Lantern trials.
- **World State Shift:** The party learns the devastating truth of the Silence: Myrraen enacted it to break mortal dependence following the horrors of Avarr. No verdict is offered, and none is permitted.
- **Key Resources:** 5th-level spells (*Raise Dead*, *Greater Restoration*, *Teleportation Circle*); the crowded stair seen under Lantern-light — investigated, never confirmed.

#### Level 10 → Level 11: The Last Council Telling
- **Narrative Milestone:** Hearing the whole telling by the stove — Avarr's pyres, the Nine divided, Orinth's choice to stay as witness — and surviving the Memory Shard of Selyra's branching skies.
- **World State Shift:** The gods are neither masters nor deserters, but fallible kin. Prophecy is possibility, not fate.
- **Key Resources:** The Vareth transit cipher copied from the Seal under Lantern-light.

#### Level 11 → Level 12: The Siege of the Last Light
- **Narrative Milestone:** Repelling the Unpassed Tide across six rounds on the parapets while Cael holds wards with staff and *Sanctuary*, then placing willing hands on the Lantern beside him and answering his question honestly.
- **World State Shift:** Act III concludes. Orinth formally loans the Lantern to the party, marks the Khar Vareth road, and writes to High Custodian Moros — sending them south to investigate the screaming soulways.
- **Key Resources:** Attunement to the *Lantern of the Last Light* (loaned, not given); party aura buffs against necrotic corruption.

### Tier 3: Masters of the Realm (Act IV & Act V)

#### Level 12 → Level 13: The Halls of Khar Vareth (Start of Act IV)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Paying the Toll of Remembrance, earning Moros's midnight consultation, proving the 38% rite-failure from the registries, mapping the screaming vats, and undergoing the projection rite.
- **World State Shift:** Tier 3 transition. The party commands continent-wide authority. The Custodians grant them unrestricted access to the sacred crypts.
- **Key Resources:** 6th-level spells (*True Seeing*, *Heal*, *Disintegrate*); descent-rite protection and an embalmer guide who knows which shafts scream loudest.

#### Level 13 → Level 14: Crossing the Bleak Path
- **Narrative Milestone:** Projecting into the Threshold of Pale Dust, walking the calcified rim of the soulway chasm with Vess's guidance, and reading the VANE-IL-984 falcon-and-calipers stamp on modern brass tapped into an ancient wound.
- **World State Shift:** The party witnesses firsthand the cosmic horror of the Engine's wound — and the invoices that prove it is being harvested.
- **Key Resources:** Stamped pipe section, northbound invoices, and a sealed spirit-glass vial (evidence; the Custodians bury it with names).

#### Level 14 → Level 15: The Unmasking of House Vane
- **Narrative Milestone:** Disabling the siphon-arrays, defeating Arch-Magus Corvin in the Reservoir Vault, surviving his relay detonation, and laying stamp, invoices, and orders before High Custodian Moros.
- **World State Shift:** Act IV concludes. The party unmasks their greatest ally as the mastermind behind the soul-drain. Seraphel flees to launch the public "Returner" movement.
- **Key Resources:** 7th-level spells (*Plane Shift*, *Teleport*, *Resurrection*); bone-seal writs of Vareth — honor, not armies.

#### Level 15 → Level 16: The Resonant Pylons of Solcaris (Start of Act V)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Working the occupied pylon field without massacring the grieving — lifting the tuning key during the hymn-crescendo, buying Pell's cipher with a proper burial, and optionally turning one council with Lantern-lit proof.
- **World State Shift:** The party is forced to act as heroic pariahs, branded heretics by the public while quietly saving the continent from spiritual annihilation.
- **Key Resources:** Pylon tuning key, Star Road cipher, and Corvin's continental orders: *"the master alone ascends."*

#### Level 16 → Level 17: The Observatory of Nythara
- **Narrative Milestone:** Climbing the Stair of Eclipses, kneeling to no vision in the Mirror Pools, and decrypting the Celestial Clock matrix: the vectors point inward-down, the soul budget line-items one vessel, the margin reads *"Nine thrones, one seat."*
- **World State Shift:** The party gains the unwavering support of the Dreamkeepers and decrypts the access codes to the subterranean Star Roads.
- **Key Resources:** 8th-level spells (online since level 15: *Antimagic Field*, *Sunburst*, *Mind Blank*); acquisition of the *Keys of Nythara*.

#### Level 17 → Level 18: The Battle of the Star Road Nexus
- **Narrative Milestone:** Storming the subterranean transit hub beneath the Whispering Wastes, holding five rounds while three keys turn, desyncing the tethers, and breaching the abyssal shaft as the sky fractures gold-violet.
- **World State Shift:** Act V concludes. The sky across Elonia fractures into auroras of spirit-fire as the ancient Ascension Engine awakens. The countdown to apotheosis begins.
- **Key Resources:** Ability Score Improvement / Feat; 9th-level spells (*Wish*, *True Resurrection*, *Mass Heal*, *Foresight*) online at level 17 as the party falls into the Deep.

---

### Tier 4: Masters of the World (Act VI — The Engine Deep & Open Veil)

#### Level 18 → Level 19: Into the Engine Deep, the Tethers, and the Fall of the Hidden Tenth (Act VI)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Surviving the Gear-Spire's inverted gravity and temporal static, severing or bleeding the four Great Ley-Tethers, refusing Seraphel's sincere bargain atop the Apotheosis Dais, and defeating **Seraphel, the Ascending Tenth** — who dies grieving, not cackling, releasing the soul-reservoir northward like low sun through ice.
- **World State Shift:** Tier 4 transition into the campaign's endgame. Characters operate at demigod capability as reality bends around the awakened Engine. Seraphel is slain; the conspiracy ends. However, the catastrophic metaphysical strain tears the Veil wide open, initiating the cosmic crisis.
- **Key Resources:** Each severed tether bought a Crucible-shield; each violent one fed the Veil's strain. Both ledgers come due.

#### Level 19 → Level 20: The Unwritten Future (Campaign Climax)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Defeating the **Avatar of the Last Mercy** at the threshold of the Open Veil — answering every claim of stasis with lived evidence from Acts I–V, then declaring mortal agency aloud — rejecting the stasis of forced certainty, and stabilizing the soulways.
- **World State Shift:** The campaign reaches its permanent climax. The party stands with Orinth before the blank final chapter of *Ain Elonar*, authoring the irreversible destiny of Elonia. No ending is canon before they choose it.
- **Key Resources:** Maximum 20th-level pinnacle; the pen itself.
