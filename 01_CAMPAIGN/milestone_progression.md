# Milestone Progression: Ashes of Elonia

> *"Progression in Elonia is measured by truth uncovered, burdens carried, and the irreversible weight of choices made."*  
> **Dungeon Master Architecture • Levels 1 through 20**

---

## 1. Milestone Progression Philosophy

In *Ashes of Elonia*, character advancement is governed strictly by narrative milestones rather than experience points (XP). 

### Operational Rules for Advancement
1. **Investigation Over Extermination:** A level is earned by solving a historical contradiction, unmasking an institutional cover-up, or securing a mythic threshold—never by clearing arbitrary monster counts.
2. **Pacing Synchronized with the Six Acts:** Levels align cleanly with the narrative spine, ensuring party capabilities (spell slots, teleportation, resurrection) do not trivialize investigations before the setting is prepared for them.
3. **World-State Feedback:** Every level advancement corresponds with a noticeable shift in how the world perceives the party, their faction standing, and their access to restricted archives and magical relics.

## 3. Level-by-Level Milestone Progression Register

### Tier 1: Local Adventurers (Act I — Port Meridian)

#### Level 1 → Level 2: Into the Shattered Vaults
- **Narrative Milestone:** Breaching the collapsed foundation of Warehouse Nine and securing the *Catalogue Descent* against feral tunnel scourges and cartel sentries.
- **World State Shift:** The party earns the professional trust of Helena Voss and junior archivist Arlen Marr. They are officially registered as licensed salvage excavators.
- **Key Resources:** Access to basic salvage equipment; first fragments of uncatalogued Pre-Silence script.

#### Level 2 → Level 3: The Witness Scriptorium
- **Narrative Milestone:** Deciphering the linguistic cipher of the *Witness Scriptorium* and proving that the ancient Ascensionists were legitimate civic reformers.
- **World State Shift:** The party discovers fresh modern bootprints in the dust, realizing they are racing an unknown modern competitor.
- **Key Resources:** 2nd-level spells unlocked; discovery of the first *Memory Leaf* containing preserved echoes of pre-Silence scholars.

#### Level 3 → Level 4: The Star Chamber & Seraphel's Patronage
- **Narrative Milestone:** Defeating the Archive Cartel strike force in the Star Chamber, recovering the intact *Catalogue Codex*, and securing the sponsorship of Lord Seraphel.
- **World State Shift:** Act I concludes. Lord Seraphel sponsors the party, providing travel warrants, lavish stipends, and letters of introduction to the Grand Lyceum of New Ilya.
- **Key Resources:** Feat / Ability Score Improvement; party gains an elite carriage and formal patron benefits from House Vane.

---

### Tier 2: Heroes of the Realm (Act II & Act III)

#### Level 4 → Level 5: The Grand Lyceum of New Ilya (Start of Act II)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Presenting the *Catalogue Codex* at the university symposium, navigating academic politics, and uncovering the first documented redactions in the public archives.
- **World State Shift:** Tier 2 transition. Characters gain regional notoriety as daring intellectual adventurers. The university deans eye them with suspicion.
- **Key Resources:** 3rd-level spells (*Fireball*, *Revivify*, *Dispel Magic*); Extra Attack for martials. Note: *Revivify* begins exhibiting strange cosmetic soul-flickers.

#### Level 5 → Level 6: The Infiltration of Athenaeum Null
- **Narrative Milestone:** Infiltrating the sealed vaults of the *Athenaeum Null* and discovering physical proof that the university deans and magistrates systematically censored history.
- **World State Shift:** The party identifies the existence of the *Ascension Engine* and its catastrophic damage to the soulways.
- **Key Resources:** Recovery of the *Broken Seal of Solcaris*; university censors initiate active surveillance and legal warrants against the party.

#### Level 6 → Level 7: Escape from the Ivory Spire
- **Narrative Milestone:** Escaping the ambush of the Athenaeum Inquisitors, surviving the flooded catacombs beneath New Ilya, and securing safe harbor at Seraphel's country villa.
- **World State Shift:** Act II concludes. The party leaves civilized safety behind, designated outlaws by the Republic of Ilya, heading into the rugged crags of the Weeping Ridge.
- **Key Resources:** 4th-level spells unlocked; Seraphel gifts the party masterwork survival gear and navigational maps to Lantern Chapel.

#### Level 7 → Level 8: The Threshold of Lantern Chapel (Start of Act III)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Navigating the perilous supernatural fog of the Weeping Ridge, pacifying the ancient boundary wards, and being welcomed into Lantern Chapel by Brother Cael.
- **World State Shift:** The campaign shifts from academic intrigue to mythic discovery. The party rests in a place untouched by the panic of the modern world.
- **Key Resources:** Access to Brother Cael's private medicinal brews (granting resistance to psychic damage and disease); character downtime.

#### Level 8 → Level 9: The Sage Unmasked
- **Narrative Milestone:** Piecing together Brother Cael's 800-year ledgers, witnessing his attunement of the ancient seal, and confronting him with the truth: he is Orinth, the Laughing Sage.
- **World State Shift:** The party learns the devastating truth of the Silence: Myrraen enacted it to break mortal dependence following the horrors of Avarr.
- **Key Resources:** 5th-level spells (*Raise Dead*, *Greater Restoration*, *Teleportation Circle*); Orinth gifts the party the *Book of Unasked Questions*.

#### Level 9 → Level 10: The Siege of the Last Light
- **Narrative Milestone:** Repelling the nightmare entity that assaults Lantern Chapel and attuning the *Lantern of the Last Light* through mortal sacrifice and resolve.
- **World State Shift:** Act III concludes. Orinth formally entrusts the Lantern to the party, sending them south to the necropolis city of Khar Vareth to investigate the screaming soulways.
- **Key Resources:** Attunement to the *Lantern of the Last Light*; party aura buffs against necrotic corruption.

---

### Tier 3: Masters of the Realm (Act IV & Act V)

#### Level 10 → Level 11: The Halls of Khar Vareth (Start of Act IV)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Entering the City of Ten Thousand Names, earning the respect of High Custodian Moros, and proving the mathematical failure of continental funerary rites.
- **World State Shift:** Tier 3 transition. The party commands continent-wide authority. The Custodians grant them unrestricted access to the sacred crypts.
- **Key Resources:** 6th-level spells (*True Seeing*, *Heal*, *Disintegrate*); specialized Vareth soul-warding talismans.

#### Level 11 → Level 12: Crossing the Bleak Path
- **Narrative Milestone:** Projecting consciousness into the metaphysical transit realm of the Bleak Path, surviving the storm of unpassed spirits, and locating the ancient soulway fracture.
- **World State Shift:** The party witnesses firsthand the cosmic horror of the Engine's wound.
- **Key Resources:** Acquisition of *Varek's Final Token*; resistance to cold and necrotic damage while traversing ethereal thresholds.

#### Level 12 → Level 13: The Unmasking of House Vane
- **Narrative Milestone:** Discovering the brass siphon-pipes bearing House Vane's foundry mark, defeating Arch-Magus Corvin in the subterranean siphons, and proving Seraphel is harvesting souls.
- **World State Shift:** Act IV concludes. The party unmasks their greatest ally as the mastermind behind the soul-drain. Seraphel flees to launch the public "Returner" movement.
- **Key Resources:** 7th-level spells (*Plane Shift*, *Teleport*, *Resurrection*); alliance with the military forces of the Dominion of Vareth.

#### Level 13 → Level 14: The Resonant Pylons of Solcaris (Start of Act V)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Dismantling Seraphel's regional soul-siphon pylons in the ruins of Solcaris despite massive public opposition and riotous mobs believing Seraphel is bringing the gods back.
- **World State Shift:** The party is forced to act as heroic pariahs, branded heretics by the public while quietly saving the continent from spiritual annihilation.
- **Key Resources:** Seizure of refined spirit-glass reservoirs, freeing tens of thousands of trapped souls.

#### Level 14 → Level 15: The Observatory of Nythara
- **Narrative Milestone:** Reaching the summit of Nythara, deciphering Selyra's celestial prophecy clock, and obtaining the mathematical proof that Seraphel seeks personal apotheosis as the Tenth.
- **World State Shift:** The party gains the unwavering support of the Dreamkeepers and decrypts the access codes to the subterranean Star Roads.
- **Key Resources:** 8th-level spells (*Antimagic Field*, *Sunburst*, *Mind Blank*); acquisition of the *Keys of Nythara*.

#### Level 15 → Level 16: The Battle of the Star Road Nexus
- **Narrative Milestone:** Storming the subterranean transit hub beneath the Whispering Wastes, defeating Seraphel's Inner Circle vanguard, and breaching the abyssal gates of the Engine Deep.
- **World State Shift:** Act V concludes. The sky across Elonia fractures into auroras of spirit-fire as the ancient Ascension Engine awakens. The countdown to apotheosis begins.
- **Key Resources:** Ability Score Improvement / Feat; access to ancient Star Road instant transit networks across the continent.

---

### Tier 4: Masters of the World (Act VI — The Engine Deep & Open Veil)

#### Level 16 → Level 17: Into the Engine Deep (Start of Act VI)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Surviving the cyclopean depths of the Engine Deep, disabling the auxiliary cooling valves, and reaching the inner sanctum of the First Ascendants.
- **World State Shift:** Tier 4 transition. Characters operate at demigod capability. Reality bends around them in the presence of the awakened Engine.
- **Key Resources:** 9th-level spells (*Wish*, *True Resurrection*, *Mass Heal*, *Foresight*); party members gain divine favor boons from the remnants of the First Covenant.

#### Level 17 → Level 18: Shattering the Conduits
- **Narrative Milestone:** Severing the four Great Ley-Tethers feeding stolen soulways into the Apotheosis Chamber, withstanding waves of god-forged automatons.
- **World State Shift:** Seraphel's apotheosis ritual is destabilized, forcing him into a premature, vulnerable transformation.
- **Key Resources:** Recovery of the *Crown of Avarr*, cleansing its ancient hubris to shield the party from divine psychic overwhelm.

#### Level 18 → Level 19: The Fall of the Hidden Tenth
- **Narrative Milestone:** Defeating **Seraphel, the Ascending Tenth** atop the Apotheosis Dais, shattering his stolen divinity, and releasing the accumulated soul-reservoir.
- **World State Shift:** Seraphel is slain; the conspiracy ends. However, the catastrophic metaphysical strain tears the Veil wide open, initiating the cosmic crisis.
- **Key Resources:** The party claims the title of *Redeemers of the Concord*; epic boons awarded.

#### Level 19 → Level 20: The Unwritten Future (Campaign Climax)
- **Narrative Milestone:** Defeating the **Avatar of the Last Mercy** at the threshold of the Open Veil, rejecting the stasis of forced certainty, and stabilizing the soulways.
- **World State Shift:** The campaign reaches its permanent climax. The party stands with Orinth before the blank final chapter of *Ain Elonar*, authoring the irreversible destiny of Elonia.
- **Key Resources:** Maximum 20th-level pinnacle; immortality / mythic ascendance; characters become foundational legends of the modern world.
