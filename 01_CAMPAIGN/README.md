## Canon Hierarchy & Authority Rules

1. **`00_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH/Ashes_of_Elonia_Official_Campaign_Bible_v1.docx`** is the supreme authority; explicit canon rulings override all draft or ambiguous text.
2. **`00_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH/Ain Elonar.docx`** is authoritative for ancient mythic narrative *within the context of in-universe historical and religious knowledge*.
3. **Files in this directory (`01_CAMPAIGN/`)** are derived master architecture, not new foundational cosmology.
4. If downstream adventures (`06_ADVENTURE/`) or session notes (`07_SESSIONS/`) conflict with these files, fix the downstream files.
5. **Protected Unknowns stay unknown (`[UNRESOLVED]`):** Never invent canonical answers for the Sea Beyond, true human origins, the destination of souls beyond passage, whether Myrraen was right, or the text of the blank final chapter of *Ain Elonar*.

*Resolved Canon Anchors:*
- **Present Day:** Fixed at **c. 1,000 AS**.
- **The Ascendants:** Pluralistic modern scholarly movement; distinct from ancient **Ascensionists**.
- **Seraphel:** Seeks personal apotheosis into divinity as the "Hidden Tenth," using "Divine Return" rhetoric as cover.
- **Brother Cael:** Orinth in disguise; an observer and witness, never an oracle or puzzle-solver.
- **Soul Crisis:** Caused by the ancient wound inflicted by the Ascension Engine, not the absence of the gods.
