## Canon hierarchy & authority

1. [[Ashes_of_Elonia_Official_Campaign_Bible_v1|Official Campaign Bible v1]] is the supreme authority.
2. [[Ain_Elonar|Ain Elonar]] is authoritative for ancient mythic narrative *within in-universe belief*.
3. Files in this folder are derived master architecture, not new foundational cosmology.
4. If [[adventure_index|adventure files]] or [[sessions_index|session notes]] conflict with these files, fix the downstream files.
5. **Protected unknowns stay unknown:** the Sea Beyond, human origins, the destination of souls, whether Myrraen was right, and the blank final chapter of *Ain Elonar*.

## Resolved canon anchors

- **Present day:** c. 1,000 AS.
- **The Ascendants** are a pluralistic modern scholarly movement, distinct from the ancient **Ascensionists**.
- **Seraphel** seeks personal apotheosis as the "Hidden Tenth," using Divine Return rhetoric as cover.
- **Brother Cael** is Orinth in disguise: witness, never oracle.
- **Soul Crisis** is caused by the ancient wound of the Ascension Engine, not the absence of the gods.
