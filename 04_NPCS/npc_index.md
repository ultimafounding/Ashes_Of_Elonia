## Canonical operating rules

1. **Seraphel must be an asset first.** His betrayal must cost the players a trusted ally.
2. **Orinth never solves the story.** Cael is a witness and questioner.
3. **Institutionalists have moral logic.** Vale and the censors fear existential panic, not cartoonishly love censorship.
4. **The Avatar is benevolent tyranny**, played with heartbreaking compassion.
