## Canonical Operational Rules for NPCs

1. **Seraphel Must Be an Asset First:** For the first half of the campaign, Seraphel must be genuinely generous, insightful, and protective. His betrayal must cost the players a trusted ally.
2. **Orinth Never Solves the Story:** Brother Cael is a witness and questioner, never an oracle or magical artillery.
3. **Institutionalists Have Moral Logic:** Dean Arcturus Vale and the censors are motivated by preventing existential panic over the failing soulways, not by cartoonish malice.
4. **The Avatar is Benevolent Tyranny:** The Avatar must be roleplayed with heartbreaking compassion, not demonic wrath.
