# Bogbrir "Bog" — Player Backstory

> *"Bogbrir" — misheard as "bugbear" — goes by Bog.*
> **Player:** Theo • **Character:** Bogbrir "Bog" • **Campaign:** [[HOME|Ashes of Elonia]]

## Appearance

Grey-furred, pale-skinned bugbear with two big upward teeth from an underbite sticking out of his mouth. Leather wraps on arms and legs, a yellowed shirt, and blue balloon shorts. Prayer beads on each limb and around his neck.

## Background

> *"I was once a low-ranking pirate aboard the 'Feystar' (I don't know about this name) but became spiritual after finding inner peace amongst the rocking of the waves. I was thrown overboard for promoting said peace and washed ashore to a beachside monastery where they took pity and began training me. The pirate's way was too strongly ingrained in me and I was kicked out for overdoing it on the monastic moonshine to emulate the swaying sea. I travelled to the free city on their advice to find work but have been drunk ever since I arrived."*

## Traits

- Incredibly superstitious — lots of prayer beads and a lucky coin round his neck.
- More superstitions to come.
- Clumsy yet acrobatic.
- Stupid enough to believe he has a tiny lump of real gold on him (given to him by the pirates) — it is, in fact, fool's gold.
- Extra gear: a banjo; uses a quarterstaff as a simple fishing rod.
