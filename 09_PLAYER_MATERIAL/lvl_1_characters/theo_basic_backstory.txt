Bogbrir (misheard being called bugbear), goes by Bog
Appearance:
Grey furred, pale skinned bugbear with two big upward teeth from an underbite sticking out of mouth.
Leather wraps on arms and legs, yellowed shirt and blue balloon shorts, prayer beads on each limb and around neck. 
Background:
“I was once a low-ranking pirate aboard the ‘Feystar’ (Idk bout this name) but became spiritual after finding inner peace amongst the rocking of the waves. I was thrown overboard for promoting said peace and washed ashore to a beachside monastery where they took pity and began training me. The pirates way was too strong ingrained in me and I was kicked out for overdoing it on the monastic moonshine to emulate the swaying sea. I travelled to the free city on their advice to find work but have been drunk ever since I arrived.”
Incredibly superstitious – lots of prayer beads, and a lucky coin round neck. 
Will come up with more superstitions
Clumsy yet acrobatic
Stupid enough to believe I have a tiny lump of real gold on me (given to me by the pirates) but it is in fact fool’s gold.
Extra gear is a banjo, uses quarter staff as a simple fishing rod
